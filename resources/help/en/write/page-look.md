# Change the color and borders of the pages
<!-- keywords: page color, paragraph spacing, line spacing, spacing presets, compact, relaxed, watermark, draft, confidential, stamp, remove watermark, background, page background, colour, page borders, border, borders, box, line style, design, double line, dotted, dashed, remove border, no color -->

The **Design** tab changes how every page looks.

## Page color

Choose **Design > Page Color**, then pick a color. The color fills each page on the screen and in a PDF. It does not print on paper, because it would use a lot of ink. **No Color** takes it away. Undo brings it back.

## Page borders

Choose **Design > Page Borders**.

1. Click **Box** to put a line on all four sides. Or tick just the sides you want. **None** takes the border away.
2. Pick a **Style** (single, thick, double, dotted, or dashed), a **Width**, and a **Color**.
3. **Distance** is how far the line is from where you measure it. **Measure from** is the **Text** or the **Edge of page**.
4. **Pages** says whether the border is on every page or just on the first page of the section, or on all of them but the first.
5. Choose **This section** or **Whole document**, then click **OK**.

## Watermark

A watermark is a word, such as DRAFT, set large and light behind the text of every page.

1. Choose **Design > Watermark**.
2. Pick one of the words in the list, such as **CONFIDENTIAL** or **DRAFT**. Or choose **Custom Watermark** to type your own words and pick a font, a size, a color, and a layout. **Diagonal** runs it up the page. **Horizontal** runs it straight across. **Semitransparent** makes it lighter, so the text stays easy to read.
3. Click **OK**.

The watermark lives in the page headers, so it is on every page that shows a header. To take it off, choose **Design > Watermark > Remove Watermark**. The other words in your headers stay. Undo puts it back in one step.

## Paragraph spacing

**Design > Paragraph Spacing** sets how much room there is between lines and between paragraphs, for the whole document at once. Pick one:

- **Default** has a little room after each paragraph.
- **No Paragraph Space** has none, and single lines.
- **Compact** and **Tight** have a little.
- **Open** and **Relaxed** have more.
- **Double** doubles the space between lines.

It changes the style that ordinary paragraphs use, so every paragraph in that style follows. A paragraph whose own spacing you set by hand keeps it. Headings keep their own spacing too. Undo puts it back in one step.
