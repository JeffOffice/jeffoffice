# Headers and footers
<!-- keywords: header, footer, page number, page numbers, running head, top of page, bottom of page, different first page, odd and even pages, link to previous, section, field, page field, date, title, insert page number, close header and footer, edit header, edit footer, margin -->

A **header** is text at the top of every page. A **footer** is text at the bottom. People use them for page numbers, a title, a date, or a company name. This topic shows how to add, edit, and close them.

## Open a header or footer to edit it

- Double-click in the top margin of a page to edit its header. Double-click in the bottom margin to edit its footer.
- Or click **Insert > Header** or **Insert > Footer**.

If the part of the document the page belongs to has no header (or footer) yet, JeffWrite opens a blank one. A blank one you double-click into is not part of the file until you type something in it, so closing it without typing leaves the document as it was. **Insert > Header** and **Insert > Footer** add it to the file at once. A blank page, such as the one left by a section that must start on an odd page, shows no header or footer, so there is none to open there.

When a header or footer is open for editing:

- The text of the page dims, so you can see what you are editing. A dashed line and a small name mark its edge.
- A new tab named **Header & Footer** appears at the left of the ribbon.
- The caret is in the header or footer. Type, select, cut, copy, paste, and change how text looks, as you do on the page. **Tab** moves to the next tab stop. A header or footer has no page breaks, column breaks, or section breaks, and no footnotes or comments, so **Page Break** and **Section Break** are gray while one is open, and text you paste leaves those out. A header usually has a **center tab stop** in the middle of the line and a **right tab stop** at the end, so one tab centers the text after it, and two tabs put text at the right edge.

The same header shows on every page that uses it. When you change it on one page, all of those pages change. It does not change the pages that use another header, such as a different first page.

## Close the header or footer

Press **Esc**, double-click in the text of the page, or click **Close Header and Footer** on the **Header & Footer** tab.

## Add a page number

Click **Header & Footer > Insert > Page Number**. JeffWrite puts a **field** where the caret is. A field is text that JeffWrite fills in. The page number field shows the number of the page it is on, so it is different on every page.

A field is one piece. The caret can't go inside it. When you press **Backspace** or **Delete** next to it, the whole field goes. You can move it, copy it, and give it a look, such as bold or a larger size. Fields that a file already has, such as the number of pages, the date, or the title of a heading, work the same way. You can't change their codes. When you copy text that has a field in it as plain text, the field is copied as what it shows on that page, such as the number 3; copy and paste inside JeffWrite keeps it a field.

## Pages with different headers

- **Different First Page** gives the first page of the section its own header and footer. A **section** is a part of the document with its own page setup. A new document has one section.
- **Different Odd & Even Pages** gives the odd pages one header and the even pages another. This is a setting of the whole document.
- **Link to Previous** is on when a section shows the same header as the section before it. Change one, and you change the other. Turn it off to give the section a copy of its own to edit. Turn it on again, and the section shows the one before it, and its own copy is let go. The first section has no section before it, so the button is off for it.

When you turn on a different first page or different odd and even pages, the new header starts blank. Type in it to fill it. Until you do, it is not part of the file.

## What JeffWrite keeps

- Pictures, shapes, text boxes, and tables in a header or footer stay where they are. You can't edit them yet, but they are not lost.
- When you save, a header or footer you did not change is written back exactly as the file had it. One you did change is written again, and everything in it that JeffWrite can't edit is kept in its place.

## Find and spelling

- **Find and Replace** and the **Spelling** pane (F7) look through the text of the document, not its headers and footers. If you open them while a header is open, JeffWrite closes the header first.
- While you edit a header or footer, misspelled words get a red wavy line, as they do on the page.

## Undo

**Undo** (Ctrl+Z) takes back the last change you made, wherever it was, in the text or in any header or footer. If it was in a header, JeffWrite opens that header to show you. **Redo** does it again. Typing in one place is one step.
