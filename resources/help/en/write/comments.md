# Add and answer comments
<!-- keywords: comment, comments, reply, resolve, reopen, delete comment, new comment, review, balloon, margin, thread, ctrl+alt+m, annotation, note to reviewer -->

A **comment** is a note in the margin about some words. It does not change the text, and it does not print unless you ask for it. Use comments to ask a question, to explain a change, or to leave a message for the next person who reads the document.

## Add a comment

1. Select the words the comment is about. Or click where it belongs.
2. Click **Review > New Comment**, or press **Ctrl+Alt+M**. You can also right-click and choose **New Comment**.
3. Type the comment and click **OK**.

The comment appears as a balloon in the margin beside the line, with the initials of the person who wrote it, and in the **Reviewing** pane, with the name and the time. The name and the initials are the ones in **File > Options**.

Adding a comment is one step, so **Ctrl+Z** takes it back.

## Read comments

- **Review > Next** and **Review > Previous** (in the **Comments** group) go from one comment to the next, and select the words it is about.
- **Review > Reviewing Pane** opens the list of every comment, in the order they are in the document. A click on one shows its words. Replies are listed under the comment they answer.
- **Review > Show Comments** turns the balloons in the margin on and off. It does not change the document.

## Reply to a comment

Click the comment in the **Reviewing** pane, type in the **Reply** box, and press **Enter** (or click **Post Reply**). Or put the caret in the words and click **Reply to Comment** in the right-click menu. The reply is saved with the comment it answers, so that other programs show the two together.

## Resolve a comment

When a question is answered, click **Resolve** in the **Reviewing** pane (or choose **Resolve Comment** in the right-click menu). The comment and its replies are shown dimmed, and stay in the document. Click **Reopen** to put them back to normal.

## Change or delete a comment

- Double-click a comment in the **Reviewing** pane to change what it says.
- **Review > Delete** deletes the comment the caret is in, with its replies. Click the arrow under it and choose **Delete All Comments in Document** to remove every comment.

Deleting a comment is one step, so **Ctrl+Z** brings it back.

## Comments in files from other programs

Comments, their replies, and which of them are resolved are kept when a file comes from another program and when you save it again, even if you never show them.
