# Put in symbols and special characters
<!-- keywords: symbol, symbols, special characters, character map, omega, nonbreaking space, nonbreaking hyphen, optional hyphen, soft hyphen, en dash, em dash, en space, em space, quotation marks, curly quotes, line break, no-width, zero width, blank page, insert symbol, unicode -->

A **symbol** is a character that is not on your keyboard, like the Greek letter omega or an arrow. A **special character** is one that the page treats in a certain way, like a space that never breaks across two lines.

## Symbols

1. Put the caret where the symbol goes.
2. Choose **Insert > Symbol**. A small box shows the symbols you used last. Click one to put it in.
3. For any other symbol, choose **More Symbols**. Pick a **Font** and a **Subset** (a block of related characters, like Arrows). Double-click a symbol, or click it and choose **Insert**.

The window stays open so you can put in more than one. The box shows the **character code** of the symbol you click. The symbol goes in the font you pick. The choice **(normal text)** leaves the text's own font.

## Special characters

Choose **Insert > Special Characters**, then one of these.

- **Nonbreaking Space** (Ctrl+Shift+Space) is a space that keeps two words on the same line.
- **Nonbreaking Hyphen** (Ctrl+Shift+Minus) is a hyphen that never lets a word split there.
- **Optional Hyphen** (Ctrl+Minus) is a hyphen that shows only if the word is split at that spot at the end of a line.
- **En Dash** and **Em Dash** are dashes. An en dash is as wide as the letter n. An em dash is as wide as the letter m.
- **En Space** and **Em Space** are spaces as wide as those letters.
- The four **quotation marks** are the curly kind, opening and closing, double and single.
- **Line Break** (Shift+Enter) starts a new line without starting a new paragraph.
- **No-Width Optional Break** is a place where a line may break, with nothing showing.
- **No-Width Nonbreak** is nothing showing that keeps the two sides on the same line.

Turn on **Home > Show/Hide** to see the marks for these in your text.

## A blank page

**Insert > Blank Page** puts a whole empty page where the caret is. The text before it stays on its page, and the text after it moves to the page after the blank one. Undo takes it away in one step.
