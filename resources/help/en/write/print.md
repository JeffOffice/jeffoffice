# Print

Press **Ctrl+P**, or click **File > Print**, to open the Print page. The choices are on the left. On the right is a picture of each page as it will print. Use the arrows under it to look at the other pages. **Show** above it picks one, two, or four pages at once. **Zoom** makes the pages larger than the size that fits. Scroll to see the rest.

## Choose how to print

- **Printer** lists the printers your computer knows about. **Save as PDF** makes a PDF file in place of paper. JeffWrite asks for a name when you click **Print**.
- **Copies** is how many copies to print.
- **Collated** matters when you print more than one copy. With it on, the pages come out as 1, 2, 3, then 1, 2, 3 again. With it off, they come out as 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3.
- **All pages**, **Current page**, **Selection**, or **Pages** chooses what to print. **Selection** prints only what you selected in the document, with the same paper, styles, headers, and footers. It is dimmed when nothing is selected.
- For **Pages**, you can also name a page of a section. Type **p2s3** for the second page of section 3, or **s3** for all of section 3. A section is a part of the document set up on its own on the **Layout** tab. You can mix them: 1-2, s3, p1s4-p2s4.
- For **Pages**, type page numbers and ranges, separated by commas, such as 1-3, 5. A range can go the other way (5-3). A range with an open end goes to the last page (8-) or starts at the first (-2).
- The list under the page numbers can limit the printing to **Only odd pages** or **Only even pages**. It counts the pages in order, from the first page of the document: the first page is page 1 (odd), the second is page 2 (even), and so on. It does not use the numbers printed on the pages, so a document whose numbers start over, or start at 5, still has its first page counted as odd. The limit works on the pages the range chose.
- **Last page first** prints the pages in reverse order, so a stack comes out in order. Together with the odd and even choices, it prints the two sides of a sheet by hand: print the odd pages, turn the stack over, then print the even pages with **Last page first** turned on or off to match your printer.
- The **Pages per sheet** list puts 2, 4, 6, 9, or 16 pages on each sheet. JeffWrite makes each page smaller to fit its place and draws a thin line round it. The pages go in rows, from the top left. A sheet with 2 or 6 pages is turned on its side, so the pages are not squeezed. The small picture on the page does not show this: it shows each page whole.
- The **Scale to** list makes each page fit a paper size: Letter, Legal, A4, A5, or A3. The page keeps its shape and sits in the middle of the sheet. Use it when the paper in the printer is not the size the document was made for.
- **Sides** chooses how the paper is used. **Print on one side** is the usual choice. **Print on both sides, flip on long edge** and **Print on both sides, flip on short edge** ask the printer to print on both sides itself. The long edge is for pages that turn like a book. The short edge is for pages that turn like a notepad. A printer that can't print on both sides has these two dimmed. Point at one to see why. The choice is remembered for next time. It is dimmed when you print to a PDF file, because a file has no sides.
- **Print on both sides by hand** is for a printer that can't print on both sides. JeffWrite prints the odd pages first. Then a message asks you to turn the stack over and put it back in the printer. When you click **OK**, it prints the even pages, last page first, so they land on the backs of the odd pages. It is not used when you print to a PDF file, because a file has no sides.
- **Paper** says what paper the page in the preview is printed on.

Pages print in the order you type them, once each. A number that is not a page of the document is passed by. If none of the numbers you typed is a page, the box gets a red edge and the **Print** button waits.

Click **Print** to send the pages. The Print page closes when the printer has them.

## Page ranges count places, not printed numbers

The numbers you type for **Pages** are places in the document: 1 is the first page, 2 is the second, and so on, whatever number each page shows. If a section starts its numbering again, such as page 1 of a new chapter, or starts at a different number, the number a page shows can differ from its place. Type the place. The line under the preview, such as **Page 5 of 12**, gives the place of the page you are looking at, so you can find the number to type.

## The paper comes from the document

Each section of a document has its own paper size and its own way up, set on the **Layout** tab. JeffWrite prints every page on the paper of its section, and turns a landscape page on its side. If the pages you chose use more than one kind of paper, the **Paper** line says so. Load the paper that each page needs.

## Paper and PDF look the same

JeffWrite draws a page the same way for the printer and for a PDF file: the same lines, the same breaks, and the same fonts. The red spelling lines and the paragraph marks never print.

To make a PDF without the Print page, see [Open, save, print, and export](open-save).
