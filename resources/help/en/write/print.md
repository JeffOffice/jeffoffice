# Print
<!-- keywords: print, printer, printing, copies, collate, collated, page range, pages, current page, preview, paper, orientation, landscape, portrait, pdf, save as pdf, ctrl+p, sheet, section -->

Press **Ctrl+P**, or click **File > Print**, to open the Print page. The choices are on the left. On the right is a picture of each page as it will print. Use the arrows under it to look at the other pages.

## Choose how to print

- **Printer** lists the printers your computer knows about. **Save as PDF** makes a PDF file in place of paper. JeffWrite asks for a name when you click **Print**.
- **Copies** is how many copies to print.
- **Collated** matters when you print more than one copy. With it on, the pages come out as 1, 2, 3, then 1, 2, 3 again. With it off, they come out as 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3.
- **All pages**, **Current page**, or **Pages** chooses what to print. For **Pages**, type page numbers and ranges, separated by commas, such as 1-3, 5. A range can go the other way (5-3). A range with an open end goes to the last page (8-) or starts at the first (-2).
- **Paper** says what paper the page in the preview is printed on.

Pages print in the order you type them, once each. A number that is not a page of the document is passed by. If none of the numbers you typed is a page, the box gets a red edge and the **Print** button waits.

Click **Print** to send the pages. The Print page closes when the printer has them.

## Page ranges count places, not printed numbers

The numbers you type for **Pages** are places in the document: 1 is the first page, 2 is the second, and so on, whatever number each page shows. If a section starts its numbering again, such as page 1 of a new chapter, or starts at a different number, the number a page shows can differ from its place. Type the place. The line under the preview, such as **Page 5 of 12**, gives the place of the page you are looking at, so you can find the number to type.

## The paper comes from the document

Each section of a document has its own paper size and its own way up, set on the **Layout** tab. JeffWrite prints every page on the paper of its section, and turns a landscape page on its side. If the pages you chose use more than one kind of paper, the **Paper** line says so. Load the paper that each page needs.

## Paper and PDF look the same

JeffWrite draws a page the same way for the printer and for a PDF file: the same lines, the same breaks, and the same fonts. The red spelling lines and the paragraph marks never print.

To make a PDF without the Print page, see [Open, save, print, and export](open-save).
