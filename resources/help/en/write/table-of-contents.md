# Table of contents
<!-- keywords: table of contents, TOC, contents, headings, update table, remove table, page numbers, leader, hyperlinks, F9 -->

A **table of contents** lists your headings with the page each one is on. JeffWrite builds it from the paragraphs that use the heading styles.

## Insert a table of contents

1. Give your headings the styles **Heading 1**, **Heading 2**, and so on.
2. Click where the table should go.
3. Click **References > Table of Contents**.
4. Choose how many levels to show, whether page numbers show and line up at the right, the dots or lines before them (the **tab leader**), and whether each line is a link. Click **OK**.

For example, with levels set to 2, a document with the headings `Introduction` (Heading 1) and `Background` (Heading 2) gets two lines, each with its page number, the second set in a little more.

## Update the table

Click inside the table and press **F9**, or click **References > Update Table**. Choose:

- **Update page numbers only** when you did not change any heading.
- **Update entire table** when you added, removed, or renamed headings.

Updating is one step that **Undo** takes back.

## Remove the table

Click inside the table and click **References > Remove Table of Contents**.

## Follow a line

If the lines are links, hold **Ctrl** and click one to go to its heading.

## Change how the lines look

The lines use the styles **TOC 1** to **TOC 9**. Change one of them in the Styles pane, then update the entire table.
