# Table of contents
<!-- keywords: table of contents, TOC, contents, headings, update table, remove table, page numbers, leader, hyperlinks, F9, gallery, automatic table, manual table, mark entry, TC field, add text, Alt+Shift+O -->

A **table of contents** lists your headings with the page each one is on. JeffWrite builds it from the paragraphs that use the heading styles.

## Insert a table of contents

1. Give your headings the styles **Heading 1**, **Heading 2**, and so on.
2. Click where the table should go.
3. Click **References > Table of Contents**.
4. Choose how many levels to show, whether page numbers show and line up at the right, the dots or lines before them (the **tab leader**), and whether each line is a link. Click **OK**.

For example, with levels set to 2, a document with the headings `Introduction` (Heading 1) and `Background` (Heading 2) gets two lines, each with its page number, the second set in a little more.

## Pick a ready-made table

Click the little arrow on **References > Table of Contents** to open the gallery:

- **Automatic Table 1** has the title "Contents" above a table made from your headings.
- **Automatic Table 2** has the title "Table of Contents" above a table made from your headings.
- **Manual Table** has lines of sample text that you type over. It is not kept up to date.
- **Table of Contents...** opens the box described above, for the choices you want to make yourself.
- **Remove Table of Contents** takes the table away.

## Add a line for words that are not a heading

1. Select the words, or click where the line should point.
2. Click **References > Mark Entry**, or press **Alt+Shift+O**.
3. Type the text of the line, choose its level (1 to 9), and click **OK**.
4. When you insert the table, turn on **Include entries marked with Mark Entry**.

Mark Entry puts a hidden **table entry field** (a TC field) in your text. A table that includes entries lists each field's text at the field's level, in the order of the text, with its page. A letter in **Table identifier** lets you keep two sets apart: a table that asks for the letter C lists only entries marked C. Turn on **Leave out the page number** for a line that should not show one.

## Turn a paragraph into a heading

Click in the paragraph, then click **References > Add Text** and choose **Level 1**, **Level 2**, or **Level 3**. The paragraph gets the style Heading 1, 2, or 3, so the table lists it. **Do Not Show in Table of Contents** gives the paragraph the usual body text style again.

## Update the table

Click inside the table and press **F9**, or click **References > Update Table**. Choose:

- **Update page numbers only** when you did not change any heading.
- **Update entire table** when you added, removed, or renamed headings.

Updating is one step that **Undo** takes back.

## A very long table

A table holds up to 20,000 lines. If your document has more headings than that, the table stops there, and JeffWrite tells you so when it makes or updates the table.

## Remove the table

Click inside the table and click **References > Remove Table of Contents**.

## Follow a line

If the lines are links, hold **Ctrl** and click one to go to its heading.

## Change how the lines look

The lines use the styles **TOC 1** to **TOC 9**. Change one of them in the Styles pane, then update the entire table.
