# Captions and tables of figures
<!-- keywords: caption, figure, table of figures, label, number, SEQ, insert caption, list of figures, chapter number, update table -->

A **caption** is a short line of text under a picture or table, or over one, such as "Figure 1: Sunrise." JeffWrite numbers captions for you. If you add one in the middle, the numbers after it go up.

## Add a caption

1. Click in the picture, the table, or the paragraph the caption is for.
2. Click **References > Insert Caption**.
3. Pick a **Label**. **Figure**, **Table**, and **Equation** are ready for you. You can also type a label of your own, such as Chart. Each label counts on its own.
4. Type any words that should come after the number, such as `: Sunrise`.
5. Choose **Above** or **Below**, and click **OK**.

The caption is a new paragraph in the style **Caption**. If you picked a table, the caption goes above or below the whole table, not inside it.

Turn on **Do not show the label** to show only the number. Turn on **Include the chapter number** to number captions by chapter, such as 2-1 and 2-2: pick the heading level that starts a chapter, and a separator. The count then begins at 1 again under each heading of that level.

## Make a table of figures

A **table of figures** lists your captions with their pages, like a table of contents.

1. Click where the table should go.
2. Click **References > Insert Table of Figures**.
3. Pick the caption label, the tab leader (the dots before the page numbers), and whether lines are links.
4. Click **OK**.

Turn off **Include label and number** to list only the words after the number.

## Update the numbers and the table

Press **F9** to update the fields in what you selected, or select everything with **Ctrl+A** first. The numbers of captions and the lines of a table of figures are made again. Updating is one step that **Undo** takes back.

## Point to a caption

Use **References > Cross-reference** to write "see Figure 2" in a way that follows the number if it changes.
