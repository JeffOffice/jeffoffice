# Set up the page
<!-- keywords: page setup, margins, margin, orientation, portrait, landscape, paper size, paper, letter, a4, legal, gutter, layout, section start, new page, continuous, odd page, even page, different first page, different odd and even pages, header distance, footer distance, whole document, this section, this point forward, from this point on, custom margins -->

**Page Setup** sets the paper, the margins, and how the pages of a **section** begin. A document has one section until you add a section break (**Layout > Breaks**); each section can have its own paper, margins, and layout.

Open it from **Layout > Page Setup**: click the small arrow at the corner of the group, or choose **Margins > Page Setup** or **Size > Page Setup**. The window has three tabs.

## Margins

Type the space between the paper's edge and the text for the **Top**, **Bottom**, **Left**, and **Right**. The **Gutter** is extra room added to the left margin for binding. **Orientation** turns the page: the width and height swap, and the margins turn with the page.

## Paper

Pick a **paper size** (Letter, Legal, Executive, A3, A4, A5, or B5), or type a **width** and **height** of your own. Paper can be from 0.5 inch to 22 inches on a side (36 to 1,584 points). If you type a width that is more than the height, the page is landscape.

## Layout

- **Section start** says where the section begins: on a new page, on the same page (**Continuous**), or on the next even or odd page. The first section's start is kept but has no effect.
- **Different first page** gives the first page of the section a header and footer of its own.
- **Different odd and even pages** gives odd pages and even pages headers and footers of their own. It applies to the whole document.
- **Header** and **Footer** say how far from the edge of the paper they begin.

## Apply to

Choose **This section** (the section the caret is in), **Whole document**, or **This point forward**. **This point forward** starts a new section at the caret and gives it, and every section after it, the changes. The text before the caret stays as it was. It starts the new section on the same page when the paper is the same. If the paper size or the turn of the page changes, the new section starts on a new page. Only the things you changed are applied, so changing the margins of the whole document leaves each section's own paper and turn as they were. Margins that would leave less than half an inch of text on a page are made smaller to fit.

Everything in the window is one step to undo, whatever the number of sections.

The quick commands on the ribbon (**Margins**, **Orientation**, **Size**, and **Columns**) change the section the caret is in. **Margins > Custom Margins** and **Size > More Paper Sizes** open this window. For breaks, columns, hyphenation, and line numbers, see [Breaks, columns, hyphenation, and line numbers](breaks-columns).
