# Use JeffWrite with the keyboard
<!-- keywords: keyboard, shortcuts, keys, hotkeys, key tips, ctrl, alt, navigate, move, select, caret, cursor, customize keyboard, change shortcut keys, reassign keys, reset keys -->

You can do everything in JeffWrite with the keyboard. This page lists the keys.

## Move and select

- **Arrow keys** move the caret. The **caret** is the blinking line that shows where typing goes.
- **Ctrl+Left** and **Ctrl+Right** move one word.
- **Home** and **End** move to the start and end of a line. **Ctrl+Home** and **Ctrl+End** move to the start and end of the document.
- **Page Up** and **Page Down** move by one window of text.
- Hold **Shift** with any of these to select text as you move.
- **Ctrl+A** selects everything.

## Type and delete

- **Enter** starts a new paragraph. **Shift+Enter** starts a new line in the same paragraph.
- **Ctrl+Enter** starts a new page.
- **Esc** closes a header or footer you are editing, and leaves a footnote you are editing for the text.
- **Tab** adds a tab. In a list, it makes the item one level deeper. **Shift+Tab** makes it one level shallower.
- **Backspace** and **Delete** remove text. Hold **Ctrl** to remove a word.

## Reach the ribbon

Press **Alt** to show a letter on each tab. Press the letter to open the tab, and then the letters that appear to reach a button. Press **Esc** to step back. **F6** moves the keyboard between the ribbon and the page.

## Choose your own shortcut keys

You can change the keys of any command, or give a key to a command that has none. Click **File > Options**, then the **Customize** tab.

1. Type in the **Search** box to find a command by its name or by its keys. The list is grouped by the ribbon's tabs, and each command shows its keys. Commands that are not on the ribbon are listed under **Other**.
2. Click the command. Its keys are listed under **Keys for this command**.
3. Click the **Press the new keys** box and press the keys you want, such as **Ctrl+Shift+B**. Then click **Assign**. A command can have up to four keys.
4. To take a key off, click it in the list and click **Remove**.

If another command already has the keys you pressed, JeffWrite says which one. Click **Take Over** to give the keys to this command, and the other command loses them. Nothing changes until you click **OK**. Then every open JeffWrite window uses your keys, and so does every window you open later. Tooltips, menus, and the table of shortcuts below show your keys.

**Reset One** puts back the usual keys of the chosen command. **Reset All** puts back the usual keys of every command. JeffWrite keeps only the commands whose keys you changed, so a command you never changed gets any better keys a later version gives it.

Some keys cannot be given to a command, because the system or typing needs them. JeffWrite says **reserved** when you press one. The reserved keys are **Ctrl+C**, **Ctrl+X**, **Ctrl+V**, **Ctrl+Z**, **Ctrl+A**, **Tab**, **Shift+Tab**, **Enter**, **Esc**, **Backspace**, **Delete**, **Alt+F4**, the arrow, **Home**, **End**, **Page Up**, **Page Down**, and **Space** keys, and a letter, digit, or symbol key pressed alone or with **Shift**. On a Mac, **Ctrl** here means the **Command** key.
