# Welcome to JeffWrite
<!-- keywords: welcome, getting started, introduction, beginner, first steps, quick start, overview, what is JeffWrite, basics, preview, tutorial, new user, learn, what works, not yet, limits, word processor, document, letter, report, pages -->

JeffWrite is a program for writing letters, reports, and other documents. It is free and open source. There is no fee, no subscription, and no account to set up. It is one of the JeffOffice apps, and it runs on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

The file you work on is called a **document**. JeffWrite saves documents as .docx files. It opens .docx files from other programs too, and it keeps what it can't show yet. See [Open, save, print, and export](open-save).

## Do these four things first

1. **Start writing.** JeffWrite opens with a blank document. Click the page and type. Press **Enter** to start a new paragraph. A **paragraph** is a block of text that ends when you press Enter.
2. **Change how the text looks.** Select some words by dragging over them. Then use the **Home** tab. Make them bold, change the size, or pick a color. To change a whole paragraph, click in it and choose an alignment or a bullet list.
3. **Use styles for headings.** A **style** is a saved look, such as Heading 1 or Title. Click a style in the **Styles** group on the Home tab to give the paragraph that look.
4. **Save.** Press **Ctrl+S**. Give the document a name the first time.

## See the pages

The page area shows your document as pages, one under another, on a gray background. To change the view, use the **View** tab or the zoom slider at the bottom right.

- **100%** shows the page at its normal size.
- **One Page** fits a whole page in the window.
- **Multiple Pages** shows pages side by side.
- **Page Width** makes the page as wide as the window.
- You can zoom from 10% to 500%. Hold **Ctrl** and turn the mouse wheel to zoom.

The first page appears at once, even in a very long document. JeffWrite works out the rest of the pages while you read, so the page count at the bottom left may grow for a moment. It shows **Page 2 of 40+** until it has counted them all.

## JeffWrite is a Preview

This version of JeffWrite is a **Preview**. It is ready to try on real work, but it does not do everything yet. Keep a copy of any file that matters while you try it.

**What works**

- Typing, selecting, cutting, copying, pasting, undoing, and redoing.
- Fonts, sizes, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, subscript, superscript, text color, and highlight color. The **Font** box (Ctrl+D) has all of them and more. See [Change how text looks](font).
- Find and Replace (Ctrl+F and Ctrl+H) in the text of the document. See [Find and replace words](find-replace).
- Paragraph alignment, indents, spacing, and line spacing. The **Paragraph** box (the small arrow at the corner of the Paragraph group) has them all.
- Bulleted and numbered lists, with levels.
- Styles. The gallery has Normal, headings, Title, and more.
- Pages: paper size, orientation, margins, columns, page breaks, and section breaks that start a new page.
- Opening and saving .docx files.
- Zoom, and showing the paragraph marks (the **¶** button).
- Printing, PDF files, and AutoRecover, which keeps a spare copy of unsaved work.

**What does not work yet**

- Headers, footers, and page numbers are not shown. If a file has them, JeffWrite keeps them.
- Footnotes and endnotes show their numbers, but not their text. JeffWrite keeps them.
- Tables are not shown or edited. A gray line stands in for each one, and JeffWrite keeps the table.
- Pictures, shapes, and text boxes are not shown. JeffWrite keeps them. A picture that sits in a line of text shows as **[picture]**.
- Fields, such as page numbers and tables of contents, show the words the file had. They do not update.
- Comments and tracked changes are not shown, and deleted text stays hidden. JeffWrite keeps them.
- You can't open or save .doc, .rtf, or .odt files yet.
- There is no spelling check and no ruler.
- Find and Replace look in the text of the document, but not in headers, footers, or notes.
- Sections that continue on the same page start a new page instead.

JeffWrite keeps what it can't show. It writes those parts back when you save, so a file that has them does not lose them.

## Get help while you work

- Press **F1**. The Help pane opens on the right. It shows help about what you are doing, such as the ribbon tab you are on. Over the File screen, Help opens in a small window of its own.
- You can also click the **?** button at the top right, next to the arrow that collapses the ribbon.
- Type a word in the search box at the top of the pane. It finds help topics and also commands. If you choose a command, JeffWrite runs it.
- Click a link to jump to another topic. Click **Back** to return to the topic you read before.

If something goes wrong, click **File > About**, then **Report a problem**.
