# Welcome to JeffWrite
<!-- keywords: welcome, getting started, introduction, beginner, first steps, quick start, overview, what is JeffWrite, basics, preview, tutorial, new user, learn, what works, not yet, limits, word processor, document, letter, report, pages, ribbon, tabs, tables, headers, footers, footnotes, pictures, comments, tracked changes, fields, doc, docx -->

JeffWrite is a program for writing letters, reports, and other documents. It is free and open source. There is no fee, no subscription, and no account to set up. It is one of the JeffOffice apps, and it runs on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

The file you work on is called a **document**. JeffWrite saves documents as .docx files. It opens .docx files from other programs too, and older .doc files. It keeps what it can't edit yet. See [Open, save, print, and export](open-save).

## Do these four things first

1. **Start writing.** JeffWrite opens with a blank document. Click the page and type. Press **Enter** to start a new paragraph. A **paragraph** is a block of text that ends when you press Enter.
2. **Change how the text looks.** Select some words by dragging over them. Then use the **Home** tab. Make them bold, change the size, or pick a color. To change a whole paragraph, click in it and choose an alignment or a bullet list.
3. **Use styles for headings.** A **style** is a saved look, such as Heading 1 or Title. Click a style in the **Styles** group on the Home tab to give the paragraph that look.
4. **Save.** Press **Ctrl+S**. Give the document a name the first time.

## See the pages

The page area shows your document as pages, one under another, on a gray background. To change the view, use the **View** tab or the zoom slider at the bottom right.

- **100%** shows the page at its normal size.
- **One Page** fits a whole page in the window.
- **Multiple Pages** shows pages side by side.
- **Page Width** makes the page as wide as the window.
- You can zoom from 10% to 500%. Hold **Ctrl** and turn the mouse wheel to zoom.

The first page appears at once, even in a very long document. JeffWrite works out the rest of the pages while you read, so the page count at the bottom left may grow for a moment. It shows **Page 2 of 40+** until it has counted them all. The word count is beside it.

## Where things are

The **ribbon** is the band of buttons across the top. Each tab holds groups of buttons.

- **Home** has Clipboard (cut, copy, and paste), Font, Paragraph, Styles, and Editing, which is Find and Replace.
- **Insert** has Page Break.
- **Layout** has Margins, Orientation, Size, Columns, and Breaks, and boxes for a paragraph's indents and spacing.
- **Review** has Spelling & Grammar and Check Spelling as You Type.
- **View** has Print Layout, the **¶** button, and the zoom choices.

Click **File** at the top left for New, Open, Save, Print, and Export.

## JeffWrite is a Preview

This version of JeffWrite is a **Preview**. It is ready to try on real work, but it does not do everything yet. Keep a copy of any file that matters while you try it.

**What works**

- Typing, selecting, cutting, copying, pasting, undoing, and redoing.
- Fonts, sizes, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, subscript, superscript, text color, and highlight color. The **Font** box (Ctrl+D) has all of them and more. See [Change how text looks](font).
- Find and Replace (Ctrl+F and Ctrl+H) in the text of the document, including the text in tables. See [Find and replace words](find-replace).
- Paragraph alignment, indents, spacing, and line spacing. The **Paragraph** box (the small arrow at the corner of the Paragraph group) has them all.
- Bulleted and numbered lists, with levels.
- Styles. The gallery has Normal, headings, Title, and more.
- Pages: paper size, orientation, margins, columns (one, two, or three), page breaks, and section breaks that start a new page.
- Opening .docx, .docm, .dotx, .dotm, .doc, and .dot files. Saving .docx and .dotx files.
- Showing what a file has, where the file puts it: tables, headers and footers, footnotes and endnotes, pictures, shapes, and text boxes (with the text going around those that float), page borders and page color, line numbers, and sections that continue on the same page. A **header** is text at the top of every page, and a **footer** is text at the bottom. A **footnote** is a note at the foot of the page that cites it, and an **endnote** is one at the end.
- Page numbers in headers and footers. They are worked out as the pages are laid out, and so is the number of pages.
- Zoom, and showing the paragraph marks (the **¶** button).
- Spelling: red lines as you type, suggestions when you right-click, and the Spelling pane (**F7**). See [Check your spelling](spelling).
- Printing, with a preview, copies, and page ranges, PDF files, and AutoRecover, which keeps a spare copy of unsaved work. See [Print](print).

**What does not work yet**

- Headers, footers, footnotes, and endnotes can't be edited, added, or taken away. JeffWrite shows them and keeps them as the file has them.
- Tables can't be inserted yet. In a table you open, you can type in the cells, press **Tab** to go to the next cell, and press **Tab** in the last cell to add a row. There are no commands yet to add or delete cells, rows, or columns, or to merge cells.
- Pictures, shapes, and text boxes can't be inserted or changed. JeffWrite keeps them.
- Fields can't be inserted. A **field** is text that the file fills in, such as a date or a table of contents. Page numbers in headers and footers, and references to the page of a bookmark, are worked out. Other fields show the words the file had, and they do not update.
- Comments and tracked changes are kept, but not shown. A **comment** is a note in the margin. A **tracked change** is an edit that a reviewer marked. Text that a tracked change added shows as plain text, and text it deleted stays hidden. You can't add, accept, or reject them.
- An equation shows as one line of text, in order, not laid out as it would print.
- You can't open .rtf or .odt files yet. You can't save .doc, .rtf, or .odt files. A .doc file is saved as a new .docx file.
- There is no ruler, and you can't set tab stops. The tab stops a file has are used.
- A section break you insert starts a new page. The sections that continue on the same page, and those that start on an odd or an even page, are laid out when a file has them, but you can't insert them.
- Paste keeps the look of the text you copied. There are no paste choices, and no Paste Special.
- Find and Replace do not look in headers, footers, or notes.
- Print Layout is the only view.

JeffWrite keeps what it can't edit or show. It writes those parts back when you save, so a file that has them does not lose them.

## Get help while you work

- Press **F1**. The Help pane opens on the right. It shows help about what you are doing, such as the ribbon tab you are on. Over the File screen, Help opens in a small window of its own.
- You can also click the **?** button at the top right, next to the arrow that collapses the ribbon.
- Type a word in the search box at the top of the pane. It finds help topics and also commands. If you choose a command, JeffWrite runs it.
- Click a link to jump to another topic. Click **Back** to return to the topic you read before.

If something goes wrong, click **File > About**, then **Report a problem**.
