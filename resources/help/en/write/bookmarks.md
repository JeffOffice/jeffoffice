# Bookmarks
<!-- keywords: bookmark, bookmarks, go to, mark a place, delete bookmark, hidden bookmarks, Ctrl+Shift+F5 -->

A **bookmark** gives a name to a place or a stretch of text, so you can jump to it, or point to it from a cross-reference.

## Add a bookmark

1. Select the text to mark, or click where the mark should go.
2. Click **Insert > Bookmark**, or press **Ctrl+Shift+F5**.
3. Type a name. It must begin with a letter, and it can have letters, numbers, and underscores, but no spaces. It can be 40 characters long.
4. Click **Add**.

If you add a name that is already used, the bookmark moves to the new place.

## Go to or delete a bookmark

Open the **Bookmark** box, pick the name in the list, and click **Go To**, or double-click the name. Click **Delete** to take the bookmark away. The text stays.

Use **Sort by** to list the names in alphabetical order or in the order they have in the text. Check **Hidden bookmarks** to see the ones that JeffWrite and other programs make for their own use, whose names begin with an underscore.

## Worked example

Select the words `Quarterly results`, press **Ctrl+Shift+F5**, type `results`, and click **Add**. Later, open **References > Cross-reference**, choose **Bookmark**, and insert **Page number** for `results`. The text shows the page the bookmark is on.
