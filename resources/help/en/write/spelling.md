# Check your spelling
<!-- keywords: spelling, spell check, spell checker, misspelled, red line, wavy line, squiggle, suggestions, ignore, ignore all, add to dictionary, custom dictionary, dictionary, language, proofing, f7, review, check spelling as you type, uppercase, numbers, grammar -->

JeffWrite checks your spelling as you type. A word that is not in the dictionary gets a red wavy line under it. The check runs on your own computer, with no internet connection, and it knows 12 languages.

## While you type

- A red wavy line means the dictionary does not have the word. The word may still be right. Names and new words are often missing.
- The word you are typing is left alone until you move on, so a half-typed word does not flicker.
- The lines show on the screen only. They never print, and they never go into a PDF.

To fix a word, **right-click** it. The menu shows these choices above the usual ones:

- Up to six **suggestions**, the best one first, in bold. Click one to put it in place of the word. It is one step, so **Ctrl+Z** takes it back.
- **Ignore All** leaves that word alone, wherever it appears, until you close JeffWrite.
- **Add to Dictionary** teaches JeffWrite the word for good. The word goes in a small file in JeffWrite's own folder, so you can read or edit your list of added words there.

If the dictionary has no suggestion, the menu says so.

## Step through the misspellings

Press **F7**, or click **Review > Spelling & Grammar**. The Spelling pane opens on the right. It shows the first misspelled word after the caret, selected in your text, with the line it is in and a list of suggestions.

- **Change** puts the chosen suggestion in place of the word. Double-click a suggestion to do the same.
- **Change All** does that for every copy of the word in the document. It is one step, so **Ctrl+Z** takes back all of them.
- **Ignore** passes this word by. Its red line stays.
- **Ignore All** and **Add to Dictionary** work as they do in the right-click menu.

The pane goes to the end of the document, then continues from the top, up to the place where it began. When no more are left, it says that the spelling check is complete. JeffWrite checks spelling only, for now. A grammar check is planned.

## Turn the red lines off or on

Click **Review > Check Spelling as You Type**. The same choice is in **File > Options > Proofing**. There you can also choose to skip words in UPPERCASE and words with numbers in them, such as 3rd. Both are skipped at first.

## Languages

JeffWrite checks each piece of text in its own language. A file can say what language its text is in, down to a single word, and JeffWrite reads that. Text that says nothing is checked in the document's own language. The file gives that language for the whole document, and a new document uses American English.

Text in a language that JeffWrite has no dictionary for is not checked. Neither is text that a file marks "do not check spelling". You can't change the language of text in JeffWrite yet, but it keeps what the file says.

The 12 dictionaries are English (American, British, Canadian, and Australian), Spanish (Mexico and Spain), French, German, Italian, Dutch, and Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal).

## What is not checked yet

Red lines do not show in tables, headers, footers, or notes yet. The Spelling pane does check the text in tables.
