# Citations and bibliography
<!-- keywords: citation, source, bibliography, works cited, references, APA, MLA, Chicago, Harvard, IEEE, manage sources, insert citation, citation style, author, book, journal -->

A **source** is a book, an article, a web page, or anything else you quote or learn from. A **citation** is the short mention of a source in your text, such as (Smith, 2020). A **bibliography** is the list of sources at the end. JeffWrite keeps your sources in one list, writes each citation and each bibliography entry for you, and changes them all when you change the style.

## Add a citation

1. Click where the citation should go.
2. Click **References > Insert Citation**.
3. Pick a source from the list, or click **New Source** to make one.
4. Type the **Pages** if the citation names them. Turn on **Leave out the author** or **Leave out the year** if the sentence already says them, or **Show the title**.
5. Click **Insert**.

## Make a source

In the Source box, pick the **type of source**. The box then asks for the details that type needs: a book asks for a title, year, city, and publisher; an article asks for a journal name, volume, issue, and pages. Type each author on a line as `Last, First Middle`. A company or group can be the author in **Organization as the author**. The **tag name** is the short name that a citation uses; JeffWrite makes one for you from the author and year, such as Smi20.

## Manage your sources

Click **References > Manage Sources**. The box shows two lists:

- The **Master list** belongs to you. Every document you write can take sources from it.
- The **Current list** is the sources in this document.

Use **Copy** to move a source from one list to the other, **New** to make a source, **Edit** to change one, and **Delete** to remove one. A preview shows how the source reads in the bibliography. Sources made in the Insert Citation box also go into the master list.

## Choose a style

Click **References > Citation Style** and pick **APA (6th edition)**, **MLA (7th edition)**, **Chicago (15th edition)**, **Harvard**, or **IEEE**. The editions are named because newer editions of those guides exist, and they are not built in. Every citation and bibliography in the document changes to that style. With IEEE, a citation is a number in brackets, such as [1], in the order the sources are first cited.

## Add the bibliography

Click where the list should go, click **References > Bibliography**, and pick **Bibliography**, **References**, or **Works Cited**. JeffWrite adds a heading and a list of the sources you cited, in order. A source you never cited is not in the list. If you cite more sources later, click in the list and press **F9** to make it again.

## What is saved

Your sources are saved in the file, in the same form other word processors read. A file you open keeps its sources exactly as they were until you change them.
