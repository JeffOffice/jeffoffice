# Change JeffDeck's settings
<!-- keywords: options, settings, preferences, theme, dark mode, language, user name, initials, ruler, snap, spelling, autocorrect, quotes, updates, beta, usage statistics, about -->

Your settings are in the **JeffDeck Options** window. Click **File > Options** to open it. It has three tabs. Click **OK** to keep your changes, or **Cancel** to throw them away.

## General

- **User name** and **Initials** are shown on the comments you write. See [Comments](comments).
- **Theme** is **Use system setting**, **Light**, or **Dark**. The change shows at once.
- **Display language** is the language of JeffDeck's own words. **Use system language** follows your computer. Other languages appear in this list when translations are available. A new language starts the next time you open JeffDeck.
- **Send anonymous usage statistics**, **Check for updates when JeffDeck starts**, and **Get beta versions (early builds for testing)** are choices for all the JeffOffice apps. In this Preview, JeffDeck does not send statistics or look for updates itself. The choices are kept for the apps that do.

**User name**, **Initials**, **Theme**, and **Display language**, and the other choices on this tab, belong to all the JeffOffice apps: change one here and it changes in each of them.

## Proofing

These check boxes control spelling and typing help. See [Type and format text](text).

- **Check spelling as you type**
- **Ignore words in UPPERCASE**
- **Ignore words that contain numbers**
- **Replace text as you type (AutoCorrect)** fixes common typing mistakes.
- **Replace straight quotes with smart quotes** turns " and ' into curly quotes.
- **AutoFormat as you type (dashes, fractions, ordinals, automatic lists)**

## View

- **Show the ruler** shows the rulers along the edges of the slide. This is the same as **View > Show > Ruler**, and it applies to every window, now and when you open new ones.
- **Snap objects to other objects** makes an object you drag line up with the other objects on the slide.
