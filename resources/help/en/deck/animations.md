# Animations
<!-- keywords: animation, animations, entrance, exit, emphasis, motion path, build, by paragraph, click step, appear, fade, wipe, play, effects, not yet, edit animations -->

An **animation** moves or changes an object on a slide while the slide show plays: a bullet that fades in, a picture that flies in from the side, or a shape that travels along a path.

## Animations play in the slide show

JeffDeck plays the animations a presentation file has. Each click in the slide show plays the next **step**. For example, a title can appear first, and then each bullet can appear with its own click. Some steps start by themselves when the slide appears, or right after the step before.

These kinds of motion play:

- Appear and disappear.
- Fade and wipe.
- Moving, turning, and changing size.
- Motion paths.
- Builds by paragraph, which show a list one paragraph at a time.

Any other effect plays as a fade.

In the slide show, a click while a step plays finishes that step and does no more. Press Left, or Backspace, or P to take a step back. See [Play the slide show and use Presenter View](show).

## You can't edit animations yet

In this Preview, JeffDeck has no tab or pane for adding, changing, or removing animations. You can't make a new animation. But nothing is lost: a slide's animations stay with it, and JeffDeck writes them back when you save. When you duplicate a slide, or reuse one from another presentation, its animations come along.
