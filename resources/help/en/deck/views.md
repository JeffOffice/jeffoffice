# Outline view and the Slide Sorter
<!-- keywords: outline view, outline, slide sorter, sorter, views, normal view, thumbnails, grid, reorder slides, move slides, select slides, promote, demote, tab, titles, text only, zoom, thumbnails size -->

Normal view is where you edit one slide at a time. Two other views help when you work on the whole presentation: **Outline View** for the words, and **Slide Sorter** for the order of the slides. Switch on the **View** tab, in **Presentation Views**, or with the buttons on the status bar.

## Outline view

Outline View shows the text of your slides as an outline, in the place of the slide pictures. The slide itself and its notes stay beside it.

- Each slide is a numbered line holding its **title**, in bold. Under it are the lines of its body text, indented by level.
- Type to change a title or a line. The slide changes when you pause, or when you click somewhere else. Each change is one step that Ctrl+Z takes back.
- Press **Enter** at the end of a title to start a new slide right after it. Press Enter in the body to start a new line at the same level.
- Press **Tab** to move a line in one level. A title that you press Tab in becomes a line of the slide before it. Press **Shift+Tab** to move a line out one level. A first-level line that you press Shift+Tab in becomes a slide of its own.
- The slide beside the outline follows the line your cursor is on.

Only the title and the first body placeholder of each slide show in the outline. Other objects on the slides, such as pictures and shapes, are not shown, and they are left as they are.

## Slide Sorter

Slide Sorter shows every slide as a picture in a grid, as many across as fit the window. The number of each slide is under it. A hidden slide has its number struck through and its picture faded. A star beside the number means a transition. In a presentation with sections, each section starts on a row of its own under a heading with its name and the number of its slides.

**Select slides**

- Click a slide to select it. Hold **Shift** and click to select every slide from the one you clicked last to this one. Hold **Ctrl** and click to add or remove one.
- Press **Ctrl+A** to select every slide.
- The arrow keys move through the grid. Hold Shift with an arrow key to select as you go. **Home**, **End**, **Page Up**, and **Page Down** go farther.

**Work on them**

- Drag the selected slides to a new place. A line shows where they will drop. Press Esc while you drag to cancel.
- Press **Delete** to delete the selected slides. Press **Ctrl+D** to duplicate them. Right-click for a menu with **New Slide**, **Duplicate Slide**, **Delete Slide**, **Hide Slide**, and **Add Section**. Each is one step that Ctrl+Z takes back.
- Double-click a slide, or press **Enter**, to open it in Normal view.
- A click on a section's heading folds its slides away. Right-click a heading for its menu. See [Add, change, and delete slides](slides).

**Change the size of the pictures.** Hold Ctrl and turn the mouse wheel, or use the zoom controls on the status bar. The slider, **Zoom Out**, **Zoom In**, and the **Zoom** menu on the **View** tab all change the size of the pictures while the sorter is open.

## Back to Normal view

Click **View > Presentation Views > Normal**, or the **Normal** button on the status bar.
