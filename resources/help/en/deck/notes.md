# Speaker notes
<!-- keywords: notes, speaker notes, notes pane, notes box, presenter notes, notes page, talking points, print notes, hide notes, show notes -->

**Speaker notes** are the words you want to remember while you present. The audience never sees them. Each slide has its own.

## Write notes

The notes box is under the slide. Click it and type. It says **Click to add notes** while it is empty. The notes are plain text. They belong to the slide you are on, so click another slide to see or write its notes.

What you type becomes one step that Ctrl+Z can take back once you pause, or when you click somewhere else.

To change the height of the box, drag the line between it and the slide. To hide it and get more room for the slide, click **View > Show > Notes**, or the **Notes** button on the status bar. Click again to bring it back.

## Use notes while you present

Presenter View shows the notes of the slide on the screen, in a size you choose. See [Play the slide show and use Presenter View](show).

## Print notes

To print the slides with their notes under them, choose **Notes Pages** on the Print page. The notes can also go into a PDF. See [Print, PDF, and slide pictures](printing).

## Notes in copies

A slide you duplicate, or reuse from another presentation, takes its notes along. See [Add, change, and delete slides](slides) and [Reuse slides from another presentation](reuse).
