# Speaker notes
<!-- keywords: notes, speaker notes, notes pane, notes box, presenter notes, notes page, talking points, print notes, hide notes, show notes -->

**Speaker notes** are the words you want to remember while you present. The audience never sees them. Each slide has its own.

## Write notes

The notes box is under the slide. Click it and type. It says **Click to add notes** while it is empty. They belong to the slide you are on, so click another slide to see or write its notes.

You can format the notes as you do the text on a slide. Select words in the notes box, then click **Bold**, **Italic**, or **Underline**, make a bulleted or numbered list, press Tab at the start of an item to move it in a level, or add a link with **Insert > Link**. A bar with the common choices also appears by words you select with the mouse. Spelling and AutoCorrect work there too. Commands that put objects on the slide, such as pictures, shapes, and tables, are turned off while you are typing in the notes, and a picture pasted into the notes is left out.

What you type becomes one step that Ctrl+Z can take back once you pause, or when you click somewhere else. Each formatting change is a step of its own.

To type on a page that looks like the printed notes page, click **View > Presentation Views > Notes Page**. The notes box on that page is the same editor, with the same formatting, and its words get bigger and smaller as you zoom the page. Zooming never changes the notes. If the notes are longer than the box, they are shown smaller to fit, the way the printed page sets them, so you can see all of them. A very long note is cut off at the bottom of the box on the printed page, and a page is never added.

To change the height of the box, drag the line between it and the slide. To hide it and get more room for the slide, click **View > Show > Notes**, or the **Notes** button on the status bar. Click again to bring it back.

## Use notes while you present

Presenter View shows the notes of the slide on the screen with their formatting, in a size you choose. You can type in them there too. The slide show's **Speaker Notes** box shows them formatted as well. See [Play the slide show and use Presenter View](show).

## Print notes

To print the slides with their notes under them, choose **Notes Pages** on the Print page. The notes can also go into a PDF. See [Print, PDF, and slide pictures](printing).

To change where the notes, slide picture, header, and page number go on the printed page, open **View > Master Views > Notes Master**.

## Notes in copies

A slide you duplicate, or reuse from another presentation, takes its notes along. See [Add, change, and delete slides](slides) and [Reuse slides from another presentation](reuse).
