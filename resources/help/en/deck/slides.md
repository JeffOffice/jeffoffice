# Add, change, and delete slides
<!-- keywords: slide, new slide, add slide, layout, duplicate, copy slide, delete slide, remove slide, hide slide, hidden, reset, section, sections, move slide, reorder, title slide, rename section, placeholder, default section -->

A presentation is a row of slides. This topic shows how to add them, change their layout, and move, copy, hide, or delete them.

## Add a slide

Click **Home > Slides > New Slide**, or press Ctrl+M. The new slide goes after the one you are on, with the same layout. After a Title Slide, the new slide has the **Title and Content** layout.

To pick the layout first, click the small arrow under **New Slide** (or the one beside it). A gallery of layouts opens. Click one to add a slide that uses it. At the bottom is **Reuse Slides…**. See [Reuse slides from another presentation](reuse).

You can also click the slides pane and press Enter, or right-click a slide and choose **New Slide**.

## Layouts

A **layout** is a pattern for a slide. It says which placeholders the slide has and where they go. A **placeholder** is a box on a layout that holds a slide's title, text, or picture. These layouts come with a new presentation:

- **Title Slide**
- **Title and Content**
- **Section Header**
- **Two Content**
- **Comparison**
- **Title Only**
- **Blank**
- **Content with Caption**
- **Picture with Caption**

To change the layout of a slide you already made, click **Home > Slides > Layout** and choose one. The text you typed stays. Each placeholder moves to the place the new layout has for its kind: a title goes to the title's place, and body text goes to the body's place. A placeholder the new layout has no place for stays where it was.

## Reset a slide

If you moved or resized placeholders and want them back, click **Home > Slides > Reset**. The placeholders return to the places their layout gives them. Their text stays.

## Go to a slide, and move slides

Click a slide in the slides pane. Or press Up and Down in the pane, or Ctrl+Page Down and Ctrl+Page Up on the slide.

To move a slide, drag its picture up or down in the slides pane. You can do the same in the Slide Sorter. See [Outline view and the Slide Sorter](views).

## Copy a slide

Right-click a slide in the slides pane and choose **Duplicate Slide**. The copy goes right after it, with the same objects, notes, transition, and animations.

## Delete a slide

Click **Home > Slides > Delete Slide**. Or select a slide in the slides pane and press Delete, or right-click it and choose **Delete Slide**. A presentation always keeps at least one slide. Press Ctrl+Z to bring a slide back.

## Hide a slide

A hidden slide stays in the file but is skipped in the slide show. It does not print unless you ask for hidden slides on the Print page. Click **Slide Show > Set Up > Hide Slide**, or right-click the slide and choose **Hide Slide**. Do the same again to show it. In the slides pane, a hidden slide has its number struck through and its picture faded.

## Sections

A **section** is a named group of slides. Sections help you keep a long presentation in order. They show in the slides pane and in the Slide Sorter, and they are saved in the file.

- To start a section, click the slide that should be its first. Then click **Home > Slides > Section > Add Section**, or right-click the slide and choose **Add Section**. The new section holds that slide and the ones after it, up to the end of the section it was in. It is named "Untitled Section". When you add the first section, the slides before it go into a section called "Default Section".
- Each section has a heading row that shows its name and how many slides it holds, such as "(3)". Click a heading to fold its slides away, and click again to show them.
- Right-click a heading for its menu: **Rename Section**, **Remove Section**, **Remove Section & Slides**, **Move Section Up**, **Move Section Down**, **Collapse All**, and **Expand All**.
- **Remove Section** takes away the heading only. Its slides join the section before it, or the next one if it was the first. **Remove Section & Slides** deletes the section's slides too, and it can't remove every slide.
- **Remove All Sections** (on the **Section** menu) takes away every heading and keeps all slides.
- Drag a slide under a heading to put it in that section.

Every one of these changes is one step that Ctrl+Z takes back.
