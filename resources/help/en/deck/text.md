# Type and format text
<!-- keywords: text, type, placeholder, title, subtitle, text box, font, size, bold, italic, underline, color, bullets, numbering, list level, alignment, line spacing, columns, character spacing, change case, format painter, clear formatting, shrink, symbol, date, link, hyperlink, spelling -->

Most slides are mostly words. This topic shows where text goes on a slide and how to format it.

## Type in a placeholder

A **placeholder** is a box on a layout that holds a slide's title, subtitle, text, or picture. A new slide gets a copy of each placeholder its layout has. An empty placeholder shows a dotted outline and a prompt, such as **Click to add title**, **Click to add subtitle**, or **Click to add text**. The outline and the prompt show only in the editing window. They never print and never show in the slide show.

Click inside a placeholder, then type. Press Esc to stop typing. The box stays selected. Press Esc again to clear the selection. You can also select a box and just start typing.

Text that is too long for its placeholder gets smaller so that it fits. Drag a placeholder to move it, or drag a handle to resize it. To put the placeholders back where the layout has them, click **Home > Slides > Reset**. See [Add, change, and delete slides](slides).

## Add a text box

For words that aren't in a placeholder, click **Insert > Text > Text Box**, then drag a box on the slide and type. A text box can be moved and resized like any object. See [Shapes, pictures, tables, and Text Art](objects).

## Format letters

Select the text to change: drag across it, or double-click a word. Select the whole box to change all of its text. Then use the **Home > Font** group:

- The **font** and **size** boxes. **Increase Font Size** and **Decrease Font Size** step the size (Ctrl+Shift+> and Ctrl+Shift+<).
- **Bold** (Ctrl+B), **Italic** (Ctrl+I), **Underline** (Ctrl+U), **Strikethrough**, and **Shadow**.
- **Character Spacing** sets how tight or loose the letters sit, from **Very Tight** to **Very Loose**. **More Spacing…** opens a window for a number of your own.
- **Change Case** has **Sentence case.**, **lowercase**, **UPPERCASE**, **Capitalize Each Word**, and **tOGGLE cASE**.
- **Font Color** opens the colors of the presentation's theme, and more.
- **Clear All Formatting** (Ctrl+Space) takes off the formatting you added.

The small button with three dots in the corner of the group opens the **Font** window (Ctrl+T), with all of these in one place.

Words can have **effects**. An effect is a look added to the letters, such as a shadow, a glow, or a reflection. A **shadow** is a soft dark copy of the words, set a little down and to the right. A **glow** is a soft ring of color around the letters. A **reflection** is a faded, upside-down copy of the words under them. An **inner shadow** darkens the letters along one inside edge. JeffDeck shows the effects a presentation already has, and keeps them when you save. The **Shadow** button gives the selected words a shadow, and a second click takes it off. A shadow is drawn right under its letters, so it never covers other words. In a presentation that has been shrunk to fit, the shadows shrink with the words.

**Format Painter** copies how text or an object looks. Select what you want to copy, click **Format Painter**, and then click or drag over what should look the same. Hold Shift while you click the button to paint more than once. Click the button again, or press Esc, to stop.

## Format paragraphs

Click in a paragraph, or select several. Then use the **Home > Paragraph** group:

- **Bullets** and **Numbering** turn a list on or off. The arrow beside each opens a choice of styles. Bullets can be a disc, circle, square, diamond, arrow, check mark, star, or dash. Numbering can be 1. 2. 3., a. b. c., A. B. C., i. ii. iii., I. II. III., 1) 2) 3), or (1) (2) (3). **Bullets and Numbering…** opens a window with more choices.
- **Decrease List Level** and **Increase List Level** move a line out or in one level. Each level is indented further. In the text place of a slide, a line that moves takes the bullet, indent, and size that the slide's layout has for the new level, so it looks like the sample lines on the layout.
- **Line Spacing** has 1, 1.15, 1.5, 2, 2.5, and 3, and **Paragraph…** for more.
- **Align Left** (Ctrl+L), **Center** (Ctrl+E), **Align Right** (Ctrl+R), and **Justify** (Ctrl+J).

A text place on a new slide already has the bullets of its layout, so what you type there is bulleted from the start. The first theme, JeffOffice, has none, and a layout that has none gives none.

Three buttons change the whole box and not just a paragraph. **Text Direction** sets **Horizontal** or **Rotate All Text 90°**. **Align Text** puts the text at the **Top**, **Middle**, or **Bottom** of its box. **Columns** sets one, two, or three columns in a text box.

## More to put in text

- **Insert > Symbols > Symbol** adds a character that isn't on the keyboard.
- **Insert > Text > Date and Time** adds the date or the time. See [Slide size, background, and footers](design) for a date on every slide.
- **Insert > Links > Link** (Ctrl+K) turns the selected text, or the selected object, into a link to a web page, to a place in the presentation, or to an email address. In the slide show, a click on a link opens web pages and email addresses. Other links do nothing yet.
- **Review > Proofing > Spelling** (F7) checks spelling. **Thesaurus** (Shift+F7) looks up other words. **Word Count** counts the words on the slides, and in the selection when you are typing. To check spelling as you type, see [Change JeffDeck's settings](options).
