# Compare presentations
<!-- keywords: compare, merge, versions, changes, reviewing pane, accept, reject, accept all, reject all, pending changes, review, another version, differences, word changes -->

**Compare** shows what another copy of a presentation changed from this one, and lets you take each change in or leave it out. Use it when a co-worker edits a copy of your presentation and sends it back.

## Compare with another presentation

1. Open your own presentation.
2. Click **Review > Compare**, and choose the other copy. A .pptx, .odp, or .ppt file works.
3. The **Reviewing Pane** opens on the right. It lists every change the other copy has, in slide order.

JeffDeck finds slides that were added, deleted, or moved; objects that were added, deleted, moved, or resized; text that changed; fill, line, and font changes; changed notes; and new comments. Slides are matched by their identity, and when that is not the same, by their title and words. Nothing in your presentation changes until you accept a change.

## Read a change

Click a change in the list. JeffDeck goes to its slide, and a frame marks the object it is about. A dashed frame is an object the other copy added. A tag at the top right of the slide counts the changes that belong to the slide itself, such as a moved slide or changed notes. For a text change, the pane under the list sets out the words: words that came are underlined, and words that went are struck through.

## Accept or reject

- **Accept** takes the change into your presentation.
- **Reject** keeps your presentation as it is here. The change stays in the list as rejected.
- **Accept All** takes every change that is still pending. **Reject All** rejects them all.
- **Previous** and **Next** go to the change before or after the one chosen, skipping the ones you have decided.

Each Accept or Reject is one step of Undo. Undo puts the slides back and makes the change pending again.

## Filter the list

Pick a slide, an author, or a state above the list. The state starts at **Pending**; choose **All**, **Accepted**, or **Rejected** to see the others. A very long list shows at most 10,000 changes, and the pane says when there are more.

## Comments

The pane's **Comments** page lists the comments of the presentation. Click one to go to it.

## Save and come back later

You do not have to finish at once. When you save the presentation as a .pptx or .odp file, the changes you have not decided on are saved in it, together with a copy of the other presentation, so the file gets larger. When you open it again, the pane lists what is still different. Changes you accepted are in your slides, so they are no longer differences; changes you rejected are still listed as rejected. Other programs ignore the extra part. A file that you save without Compare has no extra part.
