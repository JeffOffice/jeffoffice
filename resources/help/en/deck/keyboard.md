# Use JeffDeck with the keyboard
<!-- keywords: keyboard, shortcuts, shortcut keys, hotkeys, key tips, keytips, alt key, tab key, arrow keys, without a mouse, navigate, access keys, slide show keys, f5, esc -->

You can do most things in JeffDeck without a mouse. There are shortcut keys for common commands, KeyTips for the ribbon, and keys that work on the slide, in the slides pane, in the Slide Sorter, in Outline view, and in the slide show.

## Use KeyTips on the ribbon

KeyTips are small letters that show up on the ribbon. They let you reach any button without the mouse.

1. Press and release **Alt**. Letters appear on the **File** button, on each tab, and on the Quick Access Toolbar. The Quick Access Toolbar buttons use the numbers 1 to 3.
2. Press the letter of the tab you want. The tabs use **H** for **Home**, **N** for **Insert**, **G** for **Design**, **K** for **Transitions**, **S** for **Slide Show**, **R** for **Review**, and **W** for **View**. The letters for that tab's buttons appear.
3. Type the letters of the button you want. For example, press **Alt**, then **S**, then **B** to play the slide show **From Beginning**.

You can also hold Alt and press a tab's letter in one move. **Alt+H** opens the **Home** tab. **Alt+F** opens the File screen.

Press **Esc** to go back one step. Press any other key to put the letters away. Letters that look faded belong to a button you can't use now.

Press **Ctrl+F1** to hide the ribbon's buttons and show only the tab names, for more room on the slide. Press it again to bring them back.

## Work on the slide

Click the slide. Then:

- **Ctrl+Page Down** goes to the next slide, and **Ctrl+Page Up** to the one before.
- **Tab** selects the next object. **Shift+Tab** selects the one before it.
- The **arrow keys** move the selected object a small step. Hold **Alt** for the smallest step.
- **Enter** or **F2** edits the text of the selected text box, placeholder, shape, or table. You can also just start typing.
- **Delete** removes the selected object.
- **Esc** stops editing text. The box stays selected. Press Esc again to clear the selection.

While you type in a table, **Tab** moves to the next cell. Tab in the last cell adds a row.

## In the slides pane

Click the pane, then:

- **Up** and **Down** go to the slide before or after. **Home** and **End** go to the first and last slide.
- **Enter** adds a new slide after the one you are on.
- **Delete** deletes the slide you are on.

## In the Slide Sorter

- The **arrow keys**, **Home**, **End**, **Page Up**, and **Page Down** move through the grid. Hold **Shift** to select as you go.
- **Ctrl+A** selects every slide. **Ctrl+D** duplicates the selected slides. **Delete** deletes them.
- **Enter** opens the slide in Normal view.

## In Outline view

- **Enter** at the end of a title starts a new slide.
- **Tab** moves a line in one level. **Shift+Tab** moves it out one level.

## In the slide show

- **Right**, **Down**, **Space**, **Enter**, **Page Down**, or **N** go forward.
- **Left**, **Up**, **Backspace**, **Page Up**, or **P** go back.
- **Home** and **End** go to the first and last slide. A slide's number followed by **Enter** goes to that slide.
- **B** or the period key blacks out the screen. **W** or the comma key whites it out. Press the same key again to bring the slide back.
- **Ctrl+P** is the pen, **Ctrl+I** the highlighter, **Ctrl+L** the laser pointer, and **Ctrl+E** the eraser. **E** erases the ink on the slide. **Ctrl+A** brings the arrow back.
- **Esc** brings the arrow back when the pen, highlighter, laser pointer, or eraser is chosen. Otherwise it ends the show.

Press **F1** for Help about what you are doing.
