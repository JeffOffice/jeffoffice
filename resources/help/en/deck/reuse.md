# Reuse slides from another presentation
<!-- keywords: reuse slides, import slides, copy slides from another file, borrow slides, merge presentations, keep source formatting, other presentation, take slides, insert slides from file, bring slides -->

You can copy slides from another presentation into the one you are working on. The Reuse Slides pane shows the other presentation's slides, and one click adds a slide to yours.

## Add slides from a file

1. Click **Home > Slides**, then the small arrow under **New Slide**, and choose **Reuse Slides…** at the bottom of the gallery. The **Reuse Slides** pane opens on the right.
2. Click **Browse…** and choose a presentation. It can be a .pptx, .potx, .ppsx, .ppt, .pps, or .pot file.
3. The pane shows a picture of each slide, with its number and title. Click the slide you want. JeffDeck puts a copy after the slide you are on, in the same section. You can also select a slide in the pane and press Enter.
4. Click more slides to add more. Each one is one step that Ctrl+Z takes back.

The other file is not changed.

## What comes with a slide

A copy has everything that belongs to the slide: its text, shapes, pictures, charts, speaker notes, transition, and animations. Nothing is shared with the other file afterward. A change in one never changes the other.

If the other presentation's slides are a different size, the copy is fitted to the size of yours.

## Keep the old look, or take on the new one

The **Keep source formatting** box under the slides decides how a copied slide looks.

- **Cleared** (the usual choice): the slide takes the design of the presentation you are working on. It uses your layout of the same kind. Its placeholders go to that layout's places and take its text style. It has no background of its own, and its footer follows your presentation's.
- **Checked**: the slide keeps its own look. Its layout and slide master come along, with their theme, so the slide looks as it did. A layout that looks the same as one you already have is not added twice.
