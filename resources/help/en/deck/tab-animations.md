# Animations tab
<!-- keywords: animations tab, ribbon, preview, animation, gallery, effect options, add animation, animation pane, animation painter, start, duration, delay, reorder animation, move earlier, move later, on click, with previous, after previous -->

The **Animations** tab adds effects to the shapes, pictures, and text on the slide you are on, and sets when they play.

- **Preview** has **Preview**, which plays the slide's animations over the slide.
- **Animation** is a gallery of effects: **None**, and the entrance, emphasis, exit, and motion path effects. Each has its name under its picture. **Effect Options** chooses the direction, the amount, or by paragraph for the effect you picked.
- **Advanced Animation** has **Add Animation**, which gives a shape another effect, **Animation Pane**, which opens the list of the slide's effects on the right, and **Animation Painter**, which copies a shape's effects onto the next shape you click.
- **Timing** has **Start** (**On Click**, **With Previous**, or **After Previous**), **Duration**, **Delay**, and **Reorder Animation** with **Move Earlier** and **Move Later**.

See [Animations](animations).
