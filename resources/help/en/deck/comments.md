# Comments
<!-- keywords: comments, comment, reply, resolve, review, feedback, marker, notes to others, author, initials, delete comments, next comment, previous comment, show comments -->

A **comment** is a note to yourself or to a reviewer, stuck to a place on a slide. Comments do not show in the slide show or in print. People can reply to a comment, and mark it resolved when it has been dealt with.

## Add a comment

1. Click the slide, then click **Review > Comments > New Comment** (Ctrl+Alt+M).
2. If **File > Options** has no name for you yet, JeffDeck asks for your name and initials and keeps them there. You can change them later. See [Change JeffDeck's settings](options).
3. The **Comments** pane opens beside the slide, with a box that says **Type a comment**. Type, and click **Post**. Click **Cancel** to throw it away.

Each comment shows a small marker on the slide. A click on a marker opens that comment in the pane. Drag a marker to move the comment to another place on the slide.

## Read, reply, and resolve

The Comments pane lists the slide you are on. Each comment is a card with the author, the date, the text, and its replies.

- Type in the **Reply** box and click **Reply** to answer.
- Click **Resolve** when it is dealt with. A resolved comment is marked **Resolved** and its marker turns gray. Click **Reopen** to bring it back.
- Use the pencil button on a comment or a reply to **Edit** it, and the trash button to **Delete** it.
- Click **Review > Comments > Previous** or **Next** to visit each comment in the presentation, on its slide.

To hide the pane and the markers, click **Review > Comments > Show Comments**, or the **X** at the top of the pane. Click **Show Comments** again to bring them back. A presentation that comes with comments opens with them shown.

## Delete comments

**Review > Comments > Delete** has a menu. Choose **Delete** for the comment that is open, **Delete All Comments on This Slide**, or **Delete All Comments in This Presentation**. Ctrl+Z takes it back.

## In the file

Comments are saved in the .pptx file with their replies, so the people you share it with can see them.
