# Transitions tab
<!-- keywords: transitions tab, ribbon, preview, transition to this slide, effect options, duration, on mouse click, after, apply to all, fade, push, wipe, split, reveal, cover, uncover, zoom, cut -->

The **Transitions** tab sets how the slide show moves onto the slide you are on.

- **Preview** has **Preview**, which plays the transition into the slide so you can see it.
- **Transition to This Slide** is a gallery of effects: **None**, **Fade**, **Push**, **Wipe**, **Split**, **Reveal**, **Cover**, **Uncover**, **Zoom**, and **Cut**. **Effect Options** chooses the direction, or **Out** and **In**, for the ones that have them.
- **Timing** has **Duration**, **On Mouse Click**, **After** with its seconds, and **Apply To All**.

See [Transitions between slides](transitions).
