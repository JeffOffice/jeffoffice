# Transitions tab
<!-- keywords: transitions tab, ribbon, preview, transition to this slide, effect options, duration, on mouse click, after, apply to all, morph, objects, words, characters, fade, push, wipe, split, reveal, cover, uncover, zoom, cut -->

The **Transitions** tab sets how the slide show moves onto the slide you are on.

- **Preview** has **Preview**, which plays the transition into the slide so you can see it.
- **Transition to This Slide** is a gallery of effects: **None**, **Morph**, **Fade**, **Push**, **Wipe**, **Split**, **Reveal**, **Cover**, **Uncover**, **Zoom**, and **Cut**. **Effect Options** chooses the direction, or **Out** and **In**, for the ones that have them. For **Morph** it chooses **Objects**, **Words**, or **Characters**.
- **Timing** has **Duration**, **On Mouse Click**, **After** with its seconds, and **Apply To All**. A morph takes 2 seconds when you choose it. The other effects take 0.7 seconds.

See [Transitions between slides](transitions).
