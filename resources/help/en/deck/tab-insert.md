# Insert tab
<!-- keywords: insert tab, ribbon, new slide, table, pictures, images, online pictures, this device, shapes, illustrations, icons, text box, text art, header and footer, date and time, link, hyperlink, symbol, media, video, audio, movie, sound -->

The **Insert** tab adds things to your presentation.

- **Slides** has **New Slide**. See [Add, change, and delete slides](slides).
- **Tables** has **Table**, which opens a grid to pick the rows and columns, and **Insert Table…**. See [Shapes, pictures, tables, and Text Art](objects).
- **Images** has **Pictures**. Click its face to add picture files from your computer. Click its arrow for **This Device…** (the same thing) or **Online Pictures…**, which finds free pictures on the web. See [Shapes, pictures, tables, and Text Art](objects).
- **Illustrations** has **Shapes**, a gallery of lines, arrows, and shapes to draw, and **Icons**, a set of simple pictures to search and add. See [Shapes, pictures, tables, and Text Art](objects).
- **Text** has **Text Box**, **Header and Footer**, **Text Art**, which is a gallery of styles for fancy words, and **Date and Time**. Text Art is in the topic called Shapes, pictures, tables, and Text Art, listed under Related below. See [Type and format text](text) and [Slide size, background, and footers](design).
- **Links** has **Link** (Ctrl+K), which links text or an object to a web page, to a place in the presentation, or to an email address. See [Type and format text](text).
- **Symbols** has **Symbol**, for characters that aren't on the keyboard. See [Type and format text](text).
- **Media** has **Video** and **Audio**. Each opens a menu with **Video on This Device…** or **Audio on This Device…**, which puts a movie or a sound from a file on the slide. Once one is selected, the **Playback** tab sets how it plays.
