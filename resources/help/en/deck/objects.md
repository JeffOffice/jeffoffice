# Shapes, pictures, tables, and Text Art
<!-- keywords: shapes, lines, arrows, connectors, curves, freeform, scribble, pictures, images, insert picture, table, grid, rows, columns, text art, chart, smartart, diagram, equation, movie, video, sound, audio, group, arrange, front, back, rotate, flip, fill, outline, select, move, resize, drag and drop -->

Besides text, a slide can hold shapes, pictures, and tables. This topic shows how to add them and what JeffDeck does with other kinds of objects.

## Draw a shape or a line

1. Click **Home > Drawing > Shapes** (or **Insert > Illustrations > Shapes**). A gallery opens. At the top are lines, arrows, elbow and curved connectors, curves, and freehand drawing. Below are shapes, sorted by kind.
2. Click one, then drag on the slide to draw it.
3. To type in a shape, select it and press Enter, or start typing.

Select a shape or a line and use **Shape Fill** and **Shape Outline** on the **Home** tab to color the inside and the edge. Each opens the colors of the presentation's theme, and has **No Fill** or **No Outline**.

## Add a picture

Click **Insert > Images > Pictures**, pick one or more files, and click **Open**. JeffDeck puts each picture in the middle of the slide. It is no larger than most of the slide. The picture's data is saved inside the presentation, so the file is complete when you share it.

You can also drag picture files from your file manager onto a slide. A picture lands where you drop it.

JeffDeck reads PNG, JPEG, GIF, BMP, TIFF, WebP, SVG, WMF, EMF, ICO, and PDF pictures.

## Add a table

A **table** is a grid of rows and columns. Each box in the grid is a cell, and each cell holds its own text.

1. Click **Insert > Tables > Table**. Slide across the grid to pick the number of rows and columns, then click. Or choose **Insert Table…** and type the **Number of columns** and **Number of rows**.
2. The table appears in the middle of the slide, with its first cell ready to type in. Type, and press Tab to go to the next cell. Press Shift+Tab to go back. Press Tab in the last cell to add a row.

While you type in a cell, the font, size, color, and alignment buttons on the **Home** tab work on its text. JeffDeck has no table tools yet for merging cells, borders, or table styles, but tables made elsewhere show as they were made.

## Select, move, and arrange objects

- Click an object to select it. Hold **Shift** and click to add more. Or drag a box around objects on an empty part of the slide. Press **Tab** to step from one object to the next.
- Drag an object to move it. Hold **Shift** to move it only straight across or straight up and down. Hold **Ctrl** while you drag to make a copy.
- Drop one picture on top of another, and the two pictures trade places. Hold **Ctrl** while you drag to make a copy instead.
- Press the arrow keys to nudge the selected object. Hold **Alt** for the smallest step.
- Drag a handle to resize. Pictures keep their proportions at a corner. Hold Shift to stretch them freely. For other objects, hold Shift to keep the proportions.
- Drag the round handle above an object to turn it.
- Press **Delete** to remove the selected object.

**Home > Drawing > Arrange** has **Bring to Front**, **Bring Forward**, **Send Backward**, and **Send to Back** to change which object is on top. It also has **Group** (Ctrl+Shift+G) and **Ungroup**, which make several objects move and resize as one, and **Rotate Right 90°**, **Rotate Left 90°**, **Flip Horizontal**, and **Flip Vertical**.

Turn on **View > Show > Ruler**, **Gridlines**, or **Guides** for help placing objects. **File > Options > View** has **Snap objects to other objects**. See [View tab](tab-view) and [Change JeffDeck's settings](options).

## Objects JeffDeck shows but can't make yet

A presentation made elsewhere can hold things JeffDeck has no tools for yet. It shows them as well as it can, and it keeps them when you save.

- **Text Art** from an older file is shown and saved. You can move, resize, turn, and delete it, but you can't add new Text Art or change its words yet.
- **Charts** are drawn from the values saved in the file, and saved whole. You can't edit them yet.
- **SmartArt diagrams** and **equations** show as they were saved.
- **Movies** and **sounds** are kept and saved, but they don't play yet.
