# Find your way around the window
<!-- keywords: ribbon, tabs, toolbar, interface, screen layout, slides pane, thumbnails, notes, status bar, zoom, quick access toolbar, collapse ribbon, minimize ribbon, parts of the window, normal view, title bar, panes -->

The JeffDeck window has five main parts: the ribbon across the top, the slides pane on the left, the slide in the middle with its notes below it, panes that open on the right, and the status bar along the bottom. The title bar shows the name of your presentation, with a star (*) when it has changes you have not saved.

## The ribbon

The ribbon is the strip of buttons at the top. Its buttons are sorted into **tabs**: **Home**, **Insert**, **Design**, **Transitions**, **Slide Show**, **Review**, and **View**. Click a tab to see its buttons. Each tab splits its buttons into **groups**, such as **Font** and **Paragraph**. Some groups have a small button with three dots in the corner. Click it to open a window with more choices for that group.

To make more room for the slide, collapse the ribbon. Click the arrow at the far right of the tab row, or double-click a tab. Only the tab names stay. Click a tab to show the buttons again. The **?** button next to the arrow opens Help.

## The File button and the Quick Access Toolbar

The **File** button is left of the tabs. It opens the File screen, where you start, open, save, print, and export. Press Esc or click **Back to presentation** to return. See [The File screen](file-screen).

The Quick Access Toolbar is the row of three small buttons above the **File** button. It holds **Undo**, **Redo**, and **From Beginning**, which plays the slide show from the first slide.

## The slides pane

The slides pane shows a small picture and the number of each slide. The slide you are editing has a colored outline. Click a slide to go to it. Drag a picture up or down to move a slide. Right-click for a menu to add, duplicate, delete, or hide a slide, or to add a section. A hidden slide has its number struck through and its picture faded. A small star under a number means the slide has a transition. If your presentation has sections, each one starts with a heading. See [Add, change, and delete slides](slides).

## The slide

The slide shows as it will look in the show. A placeholder that is empty has a dotted outline and a prompt, such as **Click to add title**. The dotted outline and the prompt do not show in the slide show. See [Type and format text](text).

Use the mouse wheel to scroll. Hold Ctrl and turn it to zoom.

## Notes

The notes box under the slide holds your speaker notes. Drag the line above it to change its height. To hide or show it, click **View > Show > Notes** or the **Notes** button on the status bar. See [Speaker notes](notes).

## Panes on the right

Some tools open in a pane: [Reuse Slides](reuse) and Help open on the right side of the window. [Comments](comments) open beside the slide. Click the **X** at the top of a pane to close it.

## Views

The **Normal** view is the one described here. **Outline View** shows the slides' text as an outline in place of the pictures. **Slide Sorter** shows every slide as a picture in a grid. Switch views on the **View** tab, or with the buttons on the status bar. See [Outline view and the Slide Sorter](views).

## The status bar

At the left is the slide number, such as "Slide 2 of 6". At the right are the **Notes** button, the **Normal** and **Slide Sorter** buttons, a button that plays the slide show from the current slide, and the zoom controls: **Zoom Out**, a slider, **Zoom In**, the zoom percent, and **Fit to Window**.
