# Play the slide show and use Presenter View
<!-- keywords: slide show, play, present, start, full screen, from beginning, from current slide, presenter view, rehearse, rehearsal, timer, notes, next slide, two screens, monitors, black screen, white screen, keys, esc, end show, hidden slides, links, right-click, menu, pen, highlighter, laser pointer, eraser, ink, ink color, annotations, draw, keep ink, set up slide show, show type, kiosk, loop, loop continuously, without animation, show slides, advance slides, manually, custom show, custom slide show, rehearse timings, recording, use timings, timings, pen color, laser pointer color -->

The slide show plays your slides one at a time, full screen. Presenter View is a second window just for you, with your notes, a timer, and a look at the next slide. You can write on the slides while you talk, play only some of the slides, and record how long each slide takes.

## Start the show

- Press **F5**, or click **Slide Show > Start Slide Show > From Beginning**. The **From Beginning** button is also in the Quick Access Toolbar at the top left.
- Press **Shift+F5**, or click **From Current Slide**, to start at the slide you are on. The slide show button on the status bar does the same.
- Click **Custom Slide Show** and choose a custom show to play just the slides in it. See "Make a custom show" below.

**From Beginning** plays the slides your show is set up for. See "Set up the show" below. **From Current Slide** plays every slide from the one you are on.

Hidden slides are skipped. After the last slide, a black screen says "End of slide show, click to exit." When the show ends, you are back in the window, on the last slide the show showed.

## Move through the show

- **Next:** click, or press Right, Down, Space, Enter, Page Down, or N.
- **Back:** press Left, Up, Backspace, Page Up, or P. Back takes one animation step back, or goes to the slide before.
- **First and last slide:** press Home or End.
- **Go to a slide:** type its number, then press Enter.
- **Black screen:** press B or the period key. Press it again to bring the slide back. **White screen:** press W or the comma key.
- **End the show:** press Esc.

**Right-click** to open the show's menu. It has **Next**, **Previous**, **Go to Slide**, **Pointer Options**, **Screen** (black or white), and **End Show**.

If a slide has animations, each click or key plays the next step before the show moves on. See [Animations](animations). If a slide has **On Mouse Click** turned off in its transition settings, a click does not move on, but the keys do. See [Transitions between slides](transitions).

A click on a link opens it when it is a web page or an email address. Other links do nothing in the show yet.

## Draw on the slides

You can write on a slide while the show plays. What you draw is called **ink**. Choose a tool with the keys, or from the right-click menu under **Pointer Options**. On a Mac, press **Command** where this topic says **Ctrl**.

- **Pen:** press **Ctrl+P**. Hold the mouse button down and move the mouse. The pen draws a thin, solid line.
- **Highlighter:** press **Ctrl+I**. It draws a wide line that you can see through, like a marker.
- **Laser pointer:** press **Ctrl+L**. A red dot follows the mouse and leaves nothing behind. You can also hold **Ctrl** and press the mouse button, for as long as you hold them.
- **Eraser:** press **Ctrl+E**, then click a line. It takes away each line you touch.
- **Erase all the ink on the slide:** press **E**.
- **Back to the arrow:** press **Ctrl+A** or **Esc**. Esc ends the show only when the arrow is already out.

While the pen, highlighter, or eraser is chosen, a click does not move on to the next slide. Use the keys to move, or go back to the arrow.

To change the color, right-click, then click **Pointer Options > Ink Color**. The pen and the highlighter each keep their own color. The pen starts with the color you chose in **Set Up Slide Show**, and the highlighter starts yellow.

Ink stays on its slide while the show runs. Go to another slide and come back, and it is still there.

When the show ends and you drew something, JeffDeck asks: "Do you want to keep your ink annotations?"

- Click **Keep** to put each line on its slide as a shape. The pen's lines are solid and thin. The highlighter's lines are wide and see-through. They are saved with the presentation, and other programs show them. You can select and change them like any other shape. One **Ctrl+Z** takes all of them away.
- Click **Discard** to leave the slides as they were.

## Set up the show

Click **Slide Show > Set Up > Set Up Slide Show**. The choices are saved with the presentation.

- **Show type:**
  - **Presented by a speaker (full screen)** is the usual show.
  - **Browsed by an individual (window)** shows the slides in a window, with the mouse pointer showing, so a person can look through them. You can keep editing the slides while it is open. The window stays on the slide it is on, and draws the slides again.
  - **Browsed at a kiosk (full screen)** is for a show that runs by itself. It always loops, and it moves on only by the slides' times. Keys and clicks do nothing, except Esc to end it. A click on a link still opens it. A slide with no time stays on the screen.
- **Loop continuously until 'Esc':** after the last slide, the show starts again at the first.
- **Show without animation:** the slides show whole, with no animation steps to click through. Transitions still play.
- **Pen color** is the color the pen starts with. Choose it by name, or click **More Colors**. **Laser pointer color** is red, green, or blue.
- **Show slides:** **All**, or **From** one slide **To** another, or a **Custom show**. Hidden slides are skipped, except in a custom show.
- **Advance slides:** **Manually** moves on only when the presenter does. **Using timings, if present** also moves on by itself after each slide's **After** seconds. The **Use Timings** check box in the **Set Up** group does the same.

## Make a custom show

A **custom show** is a list of some of your slides, in the order you choose. You can have many. A slide can be in a custom show more than once.

1. Click **Slide Show > Start Slide Show > Custom Slide Show > Custom Shows**.
2. Click **New**. Type a name. Click slides under **Slides in presentation**, then click **Add**. The slides go to the end of **Slides in custom show**. Use the arrow buttons to change their order, and **Remove** to take a slide out. Click **OK**.
3. In the list, **Edit** changes a show, **Copy** makes another one with the same slides, and **Remove** deletes it. **Show** plays the one you chose. **Close** keeps your changes.

The **Custom Slide Show** button lists your shows by name. Click one to play it. A custom show plays the slides it lists, hidden ones too. If you delete a slide from the presentation, it leaves the custom shows too.

## Record the time of each slide

Click **Slide Show > Set Up > Rehearse Timings**. The show starts at the first slide with a small bar at the top left:

- **Next** moves on, the same as a click.
- **Pause** stops both clocks until you press it again.
- The first time is how long you have been on this slide.
- **Repeat** starts this slide's time again from nothing.
- The last time is the total.

Go through the show as you would give it. At the end, JeffDeck asks if you want to keep the new timings. Click **Yes** to give each slide you went through its time, shown in the **After** box on the **Transitions** tab, and to turn on **Use Timings**. Ctrl+Z takes it all back. Slides you did not reach keep the times they had. A time is never less than a tenth of a second.

## Presenter View with two screens

With two screens, the slide show goes to one screen for the audience, and Presenter View opens on the screen the window is on. This happens when **Slide Show > Monitors > Use Presenter View** is checked, which it is unless you clear it. With one screen, the show fills it, and Presenter View does not open. A show by an individual, or at a kiosk, never opens Presenter View.

## Rehearse with Presenter View

To try your show on one screen, click **Slide Show > Monitors > Presenter View**, or press Alt+F5. The slide show opens as a small window in a corner of the screen, and Presenter View opens beside it.

To record how long each slide takes, use **Rehearse Timings** instead. To make a slide move on by itself without recording, set **After** on the **Transitions** tab.

## What Presenter View shows

- The slide the audience sees now, large. A click on it moves on.
- Under **Next**, the slide a click brings, or the same slide when it has steps left to play.
- Your **Notes** for the slide. Click **A+** and **A-** to make the text bigger or smaller. You read the notes here, and you edit them in the notes box of the main window.
- A timer that counts from the start of the show, with **Pause** (it becomes **Resume**) and **Reset**.
- The time of day, and the slide number, such as "Slide 3 of 12".
- **Previous** and **Next** buttons, **Pen and Pointer**, **Black Screen**, **See All Slides**, and **End Slide Show**. **See All Slides** shows every slide in a grid. Click one to go to it.

**Pen and Pointer** opens the same menu as a right-click in the show. With the pen, highlighter, or eraser chosen, you draw on the large slide, and the audience sees it. With the laser pointer chosen, the dot follows your mouse over the large slide and shows on the audience's screen too.

The keys above work in either window. Closing Presenter View ends the show.
