# Design tab
<!-- keywords: design tab, ribbon, variants, theme colors, customize, slide size, standard, widescreen, format background, background color -->

The **Design** tab changes how all the slides look.

- **Variants** shows the twelve colors of the presentation's theme. Point at one to see its name. In this Preview it shows the colors and does not change them.
- **Customize** has **Slide Size**, to choose **Standard (4:3)** or **Widescreen (16:9)**, and **Format Background**, which sets a solid color behind the slide or behind every slide.

See [Slide size, background, and footers](design).
