# Review tab
<!-- keywords: review tab, ribbon, proofing, spelling, thesaurus, research, translate, language, word count, design checker, hyphenation, comments -->

The **Review** tab is always on the ribbon. It has the tools that check your words and your design before you share or print.

- **Proofing** has **Spelling**, **Research**, and **Thesaurus**. See [Spelling, thesaurus, research, and hyphenation](proofing).
- **Language** has **Translate**, which opens your selected text in a free translation site, and **Set Proofing Language…**, which tells spelling and hyphenation which language the text is in. See [Spelling, thesaurus, research, and hyphenation](proofing).
- **Comments** has **New Comment**, **Delete** (with the choices to delete the page's comments or all of them), **Previous**, **Next**, and **Show Comments**. The Comments topic in this Help tells more.
- **Check** has **Run Design Checker**, which lists problems such as empty pages. It also has **Word Count**, which shows how many pages, words, characters, paragraphs, text boxes, and pictures you have, and **Hyphenation…**, which sets how words break at line ends. See [Check your design](design-checker). **Check Accessibility** lists pictures and shapes that have no alt text, text that is hard to read against its background, and objects read in a different order than their places suggest, and selects the one you click. You can also start it from **File > Info**.
