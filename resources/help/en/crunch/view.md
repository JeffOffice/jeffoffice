# Change the view
<!-- keywords: view, zoom, freeze, freeze panes, unfreeze, gridlines, headings, formula bar, show formulas, sheet tabs, hidden sheets, headers -->

The **View** tab changes how the sheet looks on your screen. It never changes the workbook.

## Zoom

Use the slider at the bottom right, the **Zoom** button on the View tab, or hold **Ctrl** and turn the mouse wheel. You can zoom from 10% to 400%. **100%** puts it back to normal size.

## Show and hide

In the **Show** group, turn the **Gridlines** (the pale lines between cells), the **Headings** (the column letters and row numbers), and the **Formula Bar** on or off. A workbook can ask for its gridlines or headings to be off; this choice is yours to change.

## Freeze rows and columns

**Frozen** rows and columns stay in place while the rest of the sheet scrolls, so a heading row is always in view. On the **View** tab, click **Freeze Panes**:

- **Freeze Panes** freezes the rows above the selected cell and the columns to its left.
- **Freeze Top Row** freezes the first row.
- **Freeze First Column** freezes the first column.
- **Unfreeze Panes** puts them back.

A workbook that was saved with frozen panes opens with them.

## Sheets

The tabs at the bottom are the sheets of the workbook. Click a tab to show that sheet. Each sheet remembers where you were in it. Sheets that the workbook hides are not shown. A colored line under a tab is the color the sheet asked for.

## Show formulas

On the **Formulas** tab, **Show Formulas** (Ctrl+`) shows the formula in every cell that has one, in place of its value.
