# Insert a function
<!-- keywords: function, insert function, fx, formula, argument, category, search, more functions, AutoSum, Shift+F3 -->

Choose **Insert Function** on the Formulas tab (**Shift+F3**), or **More Functions** in the **AutoSum** menu.

Type in the search box a name or what you want to do ("round down", "join text") and the list shows the functions that fit, names that begin with your words first. **Kind** limits the list to financial, date and time, math and trig, statistical, lookup and reference, text, logical, information, or database functions.

Pick a function to see how it is written, what it does, and its arguments. An argument in brackets is optional, and "more of the same" means you can give as many as you need. Press **OK** (or double-click the function).

The cell goes into editing with the formula begun: **=SUM()**, with the cursor inside the brackets, ready for the arguments. If you are already typing a formula, the function goes in at the cursor. Only the functions JeffCrunch can work out are listed.

## Messages on cells

A cell that has a rule for what may be entered can also have a message (a title and a sentence). It shows beside the cell while the cell is the one selected. Lists that read cells of another sheet work too.
