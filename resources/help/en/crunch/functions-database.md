# Database functions
<!-- keywords: functions, DAVERAGE, DCOUNT, DCOUNTA, DGET, DMAX, DMIN, DPRODUCT, DSTDEV, DSTDEVP, DSUM, DVAR, DVARP -->

These are the database functions that JeffCrunch can work out. Each one shows how it is written, what it does, and what each of its arguments is. An argument in brackets is optional, and "..." means you can give as many more of the last one as you need. To try one, see [Pick a function](insert-function). While you type a function in a cell, a small tip under the cell shows its arguments, and a click on the function's name in the tip opens its entry here.

## DAVERAGE

`DAVERAGE(database, field, criteria)`

Averages the numbers in a column of a list for the rows that meet the criteria.

- `database`: The block of cells that holds the list, with its column names in the first row.
- `field`: The column to use: its name in quotes, or its number counting the first column as 1.
- `criteria`: What to look for: a number, a text, or a test such as ">5".
