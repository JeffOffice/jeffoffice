# Spelling, thesaurus, research, and hyphenation
<!-- keywords: spell check, spelling, spellcheck, thesaurus, synonyms, research, dictionary, proofing language, hyphenation, hyphenate, translate, autocorrect, misspelled, red squiggle, language, grammar, grammar check, style, blue line, dotted line, check grammar as you type -->

The **Review** tab has tools that help you get your words right.

## Check spelling as you type

JeffPub puts a red wavy line under a word it does not know. Right-click the word to pick a suggestion, choose **Ignore All** (for this session), or choose **Add to Dictionary**.

To turn this off, click the arrow beside **Review > Proofing > Spelling** and click **Check Spelling as You Type**. The same choice is in **File > Options > Proofing**. It is one choice for all the JeffOffice apps and every window, and JeffPub remembers it for the next time you start.

## Check grammar as you type

JeffPub also looks for clear grammar and style mistakes in English text, in text boxes, shapes, and tables. An issue gets a blue dotted line under it, and a misspelled word keeps its red wavy line. Right-click the issue to see what is wrong and to pick a suggestion, or choose **Ignore This Issue** or **Ignore All**. Only English is checked: text in another language, and text marked "do not check", is skipped. To turn it off, click **File > Options > Proofing** and clear **Check grammar as you type**. It is one choice for all the JeffOffice apps.

**Choose what it looks for.** Click **File > Options > Proofing > Grammar settings** to pick the kinds of issues: **Grammar** (verbs that do not agree, the wrong form of a verb, "a" and "an"), **Punctuation** (extra or missing spaces, commas, and hyphens), **Capitalization**, **Mixed-up words** (words that sound alike), **Repetition**, and **Style**. **Style** is off until you turn it on. It suggests, and it is not about mistakes: sentences of more than 40 words, the passive voice, three sentences in a row that begin with the same word, and wordy phrases ("in order to", "due to the fact that"). A change applies at once to the blue lines and to the Spelling pane (F7), in every open window and every JeffOffice app.

## Check the whole publication

1. Click **Review > Proofing > Spelling**, or press F7.
2. The **Check Spelling** dialog shows the first problem word under **Not in dictionary**, with **Suggestions** below it.
3. Pick a suggestion, or type your own in **Change to**.
4. Click **Change** for this word or **Change All** for every match. Click **Ignore** to skip it, **Ignore All** to skip it for this session, or **Add** to save it in your own word list.

JeffPub checks every text box, shape, and table, starting on the first page. When **Check grammar as you type** is on, the check goes on to the grammar issues after the spelling, and shows what is wrong in place of **Not in dictionary**. It says when it is done. It skips words in UPPERCASE and words with numbers. You can change that on the **Proofing** tab of **File > Options** (the **JeffPub Options** window).

## Set the language

Spelling and automatic hyphenation follow the language of the text. Text with no language set is checked as English (United States). JeffPub has dictionaries for English (United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia), Spanish (21 regions, from Argentina to Venezuela, including Mexico and Spain), French, German, Italian, Dutch, and Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal).

1. Select the text.
2. Click **Review > Language > Set Proofing Language…**
3. Choose a language. Choose **Do not check spelling** for text such as names or codes.

## Find a better word

- **Thesaurus:** click in a word and click **Review > Proofing > Thesaurus** (Shift+F7). Pick a synonym, which is a word with a similar meaning. Click **Insert** to replace your word. **Look Up** searches for the synonym you picked.
- **Research:** click **Review > Proofing > Research** (Alt+F7) to open the **Research** pane. Type a word in **Search for** and click **Look Up**.

Both use a thesaurus built into JeffPub, in English only. Nothing you type is sent online.

## Hyphenation

Hyphenation breaks long words with a hyphen at the end of a line, so lines look even. New text boxes hyphenate by default. To change that for new boxes, see **Automatically hyphenate in new text boxes** on the **Advanced** tab of **File > Options** (the **JeffPub Options** window).

To change one story, click in its text box and click **Review > Check > Hyphenation…** (Ctrl+Shift+H). You can also use **Text Box > Text > Hyphenation**. In the **Hyphenation** dialog:

- **Automatically hyphenate this story** turns it on or off.
- **Hyphenation zone** sets how much empty space a line may have at its end before JeffPub breaks a word instead.
- **Insert Optional Hyphen** adds a hyphen at the caret. It shows only if the word breaks there.
- **Add Optional Hyphens** adds one at every place a long word can break.

## Translate text

Select some text and click **Review > Language > Translate**. Choose a language and click **OK**. JeffPub then opens your web browser at LibreTranslate, a free, open-source translation site, with your text. Because the text goes to that site, do not use it for private words.
