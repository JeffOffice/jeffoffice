# Use the ruler
<!-- keywords: ruler, indent, indents, first line indent, hanging indent, left indent, right indent, tab, tab stop, tab stops, margin, margins, drag, units, inches, centimeters, show ruler, hide ruler, centimetres -->

The **ruler** above the pages shows the margins, the indents of the paragraph the caret is in, and its tab stops. Drag a marker to change it. **View > Ruler** shows or hides it. **View > Vertical Ruler** adds a second ruler on the left of the pages.

The white part of the ruler is the text area of the page the caret is on; the grey ends are the left and right margins. The numbers count from the left margin, in the units you set in File > Options (inches, centimetres, millimetres, points, or picas).

## The vertical ruler

The **vertical ruler** runs down the left of the pages. For each page you can see, the grey ends are the top and bottom margins, and the white part is the text area. The numbers count from the top of the text area, up into the top margin and down toward the bottom one. You cannot drag anything on it yet. It goes away in Outline view and in Focus.

## Indents

- The **first line indent** is the top marker. Drag it to move the first line of the paragraph.
- The **hanging indent** is the bottom triangle. Drag it to move every line but the first.
- The **left indent** is the small box under the triangle. Drag it to move the first line and the other lines together.
- The **right indent** is the triangle at the right end.

If several paragraphs are selected, each one moves by the same amount. Hold **Alt** while dragging to move in finer steps; otherwise the markers jump to marks of the ruler.

## Tab stops

The box at the left end of the ruler shows the kind of tab stop a click adds: **left**, **centre**, **right**, or **decimal**. Click the box to change the kind.

- Click the ruler to add a tab stop of that kind.
- Drag a tab stop to move it. Its leader (the dots or line that fill the space before it) goes with it.
- Drag a tab stop up or down off the ruler to take it away.

A paragraph keeps up to 64 tab stops.

## Margins

Drag the line where the grey margin meets the white text area to change the **left margin** or **right margin** of the section the caret is in. The text and the indents move with it.

Every change you make on the ruler is one step to undo.
