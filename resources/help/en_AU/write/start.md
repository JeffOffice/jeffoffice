# Welcome to JeffWrite
<!-- keywords: welcome, getting started, introduction, beginner, first steps, quick start, overview, what is JeffWrite, basics, beta, tutorial, new user, learn, what works, not yet, limits, word processor, document, letter, report, pages, ribbon, tabs, tables, headers, footers, footnotes, pictures, comments, tracked changes, fields, doc, docx -->

JeffWrite is a program for writing letters, reports, and other documents. It is free and open source. There is no fee, no subscription, and no account to set up. It is one of the JeffOffice apps, and it runs on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

The file you work on is called a **document**. JeffWrite saves documents as .docx files. It opens .docx files from other programs too, and older .doc files. It keeps what it can't edit yet. See [Open, save, print, and export](open-save).

## Do these four things first

1. **Start writing.** JeffWrite opens with a blank document. Click the page and type. Press **Enter** to start a new paragraph. A **paragraph** is a block of text that ends when you press Enter.
2. **Change how the text looks.** Select some words by dragging over them. Then use the **Home** tab. Make them bold, change the size, or pick a colour. To change a whole paragraph, click in it and choose an alignment or a bullet list.
3. **Use styles for headings.** A **style** is a saved look, such as Heading 1 or Title. Click a style in the **Styles** group on the Home tab to give the paragraph that look.
4. **Save.** Press **Ctrl+S**. Give the document a name the first time.

## See the pages

The page area shows your document as pages, one under another, on a grey background. To change the view, use the **View** tab or the zoom slider at the bottom right.

- **100%** shows the page at its normal size.
- **One Page** fits a whole page in the window.
- **Multiple Pages** shows pages side by side.
- **Page Width** makes the page as wide as the window.
- You can zoom from 10% to 500%. Hold **Ctrl** and turn the mouse wheel to zoom.

The first page appears at once, even in a very long document. JeffWrite works out the rest of the pages while you read, so the page count at the bottom left may grow for a moment. It shows **Page 2 of 40+** until it has counted them all. The word count is beside it.

## Where things are

The **ribbon** is the band of buttons across the top. Each tab holds groups of buttons.

- **Home** has Clipboard (cut, copy, and paste), Font, Paragraph, Styles, and Editing, which is Find and Replace.
- **Insert** has Table, Pictures, Page Break, Header, Footer, Bookmark, Cross-reference, and Field. A picture you click gets the **Picture Format** tab. While a header or footer is open for editing, the **Header & Footer** tab appears.
- **Layout** has Margins, Orientation, Size, Columns, and Breaks (and the **Page Setup** window, from the corner of the group), and boxes for a paragraph's indents and spacing.
- **References** has the Table of Contents, footnotes and endnotes, cross-references, and Update Field.
- **Review** has Spelling, Check Spelling as You Type, Check Grammar as You Type, comments, Track Changes, and Accept and Reject.
- **View** has Print Layout, the **¶** button, the **Ruler**, and the zoom choices.

Click **File** at the top left for New, Open, Save, Print, and Export.

## JeffWrite is a beta

This version of JeffWrite is a **beta**. It is ready for real work, but it does not do everything yet. About three of every four pages lay out within a small difference of the original program on documents JeffWrite was never tuned on, so a few lines or pages can still break in a different place. Keep a copy of any file that matters.

**What works**

- Typing, selecting, cutting, copying, pasting, undoing, and redoing.
- Fonts, sizes, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, subscript, superscript, text colour, and highlight colour. The **Font** box (Ctrl+D) has all of them and more. See [Change how text looks](font).
- Find and Replace (Ctrl+F and Ctrl+H) in the text of the document, including the text in tables. See [Find and replace words](find-replace).
- Paragraph alignment, indents, spacing, and line spacing. The **Paragraph** box (the small arrow at the corner of the Paragraph group) has them all.
- Bulleted and numbered lists, with levels.
- Styles. The gallery has Normal, headings, Title, and more. The **Styles** pane (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+S) lists them all, and you can make a style from your text and change any style (see [Use styles](styles)).
- Copy Formatting, which copies the look of some text onto other text (see [Copy a look with Copy Formatting](format-painter)).
- Go To (Ctrl+G) a page, section, line, bookmark, footnote, endnote, comment, field, table, graphic, equation, or heading, and dragging selected text to move it, or Ctrl-dragging it to copy it (see [Go to a place and move text](go-to-and-move-text)).
- Pages: paper size, orientation, margins, columns (one, two, or three), page breaks, and section breaks that start a new page. **Layout > Page Setup** sets the margins, paper, and layout of a section or of the whole document (see [Set up the page](page-setup)).
- Pictures: **Insert > Pictures** puts in a picture from a file or the clipboard; you can size it with its handles, move it, wrap the text round it, and give it alt text (see [Add and change pictures](pictures)).
- The ruler: drag the indents, tab stops, and margins (see [Use the ruler](ruler)).
- Tables: **Insert > Table** makes one, and the **Table Design** and **Table Layout** tabs add and take away rows and columns, merge and split cells, put lines and shading on cells, pick a table style, set the size of rows and columns, and set where the words sit in a cell. In a table, **Tab** goes to the next cell, and **Tab** in the last cell adds a row. See [Make and change tables](tables).
- Headers and footers: double-click in the top or bottom margin of a page, or use **Insert > Header** and **Insert > Footer**, then type, add a page number, and choose a different first page, different odd and even pages, or a link to the section before. See [Headers and footers](headers-footers).
- Footnotes and endnotes: click in a note to edit it where it stands, and use **References > Insert Footnote** (Ctrl+Alt+F) and **Insert Endnote** (Ctrl+Alt+D) to add one. See [Footnotes and endnotes](footnotes-endnotes).
- Opening .docx, .docm, .dotx, .dotm, .doc, .dot, .rtf, .odt, .ott, and .fodt files. Saving .docx, .docm, .dotx, .dotm, .odt, .rtf, and .txt files. See [Open and save OpenDocument files](open-odt) and [Open RTF files](open-rtf).
- Showing what a file has, where the file puts it: tables, headers and footers, footnotes and endnotes, pictures, shapes, and text boxes (with the text going around those that float), page borders and page colour, line numbers, and sections that continue on the same page. A **header** is text at the top of every page, and a **footer** is text at the bottom. A **footnote** is a note at the foot of the page that cites it, and an **endnote** is one at the end.
- Page numbers in headers and footers. They are worked out as the pages are laid out, and so is the number of pages. **Insert Page Number** is on the **Header & Footer** tab.
- Zoom, and showing the paragraph marks (the **¶** button).
- Spelling: red lines as you type, suggestions when you right-click, and the Spelling pane (**F7**). See [Check your spelling](spelling).
- Printing, with a preview, copies, and page ranges, PDF files, and automatic recovery, which keeps a spare copy of unsaved work. See [Print](print).

**What does not work yet**

- The pictures, shapes, and tables in a header, a footer, or a note are kept, but not edited yet.
- Tables can't be drawn with a pen yet, turned into text, or given a formula, and a table can't be put in a cell of another table.
- Shapes and text boxes can't be inserted or changed, and pictures can't be cropped or linked to their files. JeffWrite keeps them.
- A **field** is text that the file fills in, such as a date or a table of contents. **Insert > Field** puts one in, and **F9** works it out again. JeffWrite works out the kinds listed in [Fields](fields), such as page numbers, dates, references, and figure numbers. Any other kind shows the words the file had, and it does not update.
- A **comment** is a note in the margin. A **tracked change** is an edit that a reviewer marked. JeffWrite shows both, and you can add, answer, accept, and reject them. See [Add and answer comments](comments) and [Track changes, and accept or reject them](track-changes). Edits to tables, numbering, sections, headers, footers, and notes are not tracked yet.
- An equation shows as one line of text, in order, not laid out as it would print.
- You can't save .doc files. A .doc file is saved as a new .docx file, and a .fodt file as a new .odt file.
- The ruler is across the top only, and tab stops are set on it (there is no Tabs window yet).
- A section break you insert starts a new page. The sections that continue on the same page, and those that start on an odd or an even page, are laid out when a file has them, but you can't insert them.
- Paste keeps the look of the text you copied. There are no paste choices, and no Paste Special.
- Find and Replace do not look in headers, footers, or notes.
- Print Layout is the only view.

JeffWrite keeps what it can't edit or show. It writes those parts back when you save, so a file that has them does not lose them.

## Get help while you work

- Press **F1**. The Help pane opens on the right. It shows help about what you are doing, such as the ribbon tab you are on. Over the File screen, Help opens in a small window of its own.
- You can also click the **?** button at the top right, next to the arrow that collapses the ribbon.
- Type a word in the search box at the top of the pane. It finds help topics and also commands. If you choose a command, JeffWrite runs it.
- Click a link to jump to another topic. Click **Back** to return to the topic you read before.

If something goes wrong, click **File > About**, then **Report a problem**.
