# Picture Format tab
<!-- keywords: picture format tab, picture tools, crop, recolor, corrections, brightness, caption, picture styles, ribbon, contextual tab -->

The **Picture Format** tab appears when you select a picture. Use it to change the picture, fix its colours, crop it, and give it a style.

- **Insert**: change a picture, swap two pictures, or tidy pictures parked beside the page. See [Add and adjust pictures](pictures).
- **Adjust**: change brightness, contrast, and colour, set transparency, compress the picture, or reset it (**Reset Picture** or **Reset Picture & Size**), and turn an SVG picture into shapes. See [Add and adjust pictures](pictures).
- **Picture Styles**: pick a ready-made look from the gallery, add a border, add effects (shadow, reflection, glow, soft edges, bevel, and 3-D rotation), a caption, or a shape. See [Add and adjust pictures](pictures).
- **Arrange**: wrap text, change the stacking order, group, align, and rotate. See [Move, align, group, and layer objects](arrange) and [Wrap text around objects](wrap).
- **Crop**: crop the picture, cut it to an aspect ratio, fit it inside its frame, fill the frame with it, or clear the crop. See [Add and adjust pictures](pictures).
- **Size**: type an exact height and width. See [Move, align, group, and layer objects](arrange).
