# The File screen

The **File** button, left of the tabs, opens the File screen. It covers the window and holds everything that works on the whole file: starting, opening, saving, printing, and exporting. Press Esc, or click **Back to presentation**, to return to your slides. **Alt+F** also opens it.

On the left is a list. Some entries open a page, and some do their job at once.

## Start

- **New** shows a **Blank Presentation** to click. See [Open and save presentations](open-save).
- **Open** has **Browse** to pick a file, **Recover Unsaved Work** to bring back copies that automatic recovery kept, and lists of **Pinned** and **Recent Presentations**. See [Open and save presentations](open-save) and [Get back unsaved work](recover).

## This presentation

- **Info** shows the facts about the presentation and protects it before you share it. See the Info section below.
- **Save** and **Save As** save the file, and the screen closes when they are done. See [Open and save presentations](open-save).
- **Print** is the Print page, with a preview. See [Print, PDF, and slide pictures](printing).
- **Export** has **Export to PDF**, **Export Slide as Picture**, and **Make an Email**. See [Print, PDF, and slide pictures](printing).
- **Close** closes the presentation's window. JeffDeck asks first whether to save changes.

## Info

Click **Info** to see the facts about your presentation, and to protect it before you share it.

At the top, the page shows where the file is saved. Click **Show in Folder** to open that folder. Click **Copy Path** to copy the path, so you can paste it into an email or a chat.

### Properties

Properties are facts about the presentation, such as its title and who wrote it. The page has a box for each one: **Title**, **Author**, **Subject**, **Keywords**, **Categories**, **Comments**, **Company**, and **Manager**. Type in a box and press **Tab** or **Enter**. JeffDeck keeps the change, and saves it the next time you save the presentation. **Ctrl+Z** takes it back.

Under the boxes, the page shows when the presentation was made, saved, and printed, and how many slides it has, how many are hidden, and how many have speaker notes. Click **Custom Properties** to make properties of your own, such as **Project** or **Client**. Click **Add**, type a name, choose what kind of value it is (text, a whole number, a number, yes or no, or a date), and type the value.

### Mark as Final

Click **Mark as Final** to tell readers the presentation is finished. JeffDeck saves the presentation and then holds it for reading only. When someone opens it, the title bar says it is read-only, and a note tells them it was marked as final. This is a gentle way to say "please don't change this". It is not a lock, because anyone can choose **Marked as Final** again to edit it. An **.odp** file can't hold the mark, so save the presentation as a **.pptx** file first.

### Encrypt with Password

Click **Encrypt with Password** to ask for a password whenever the file is opened. Type the password twice, and click **Protect**. The file is scrambled when you save it, so nobody can read it without the password.

- A password **can't be found again** if you forget it. Keep it somewhere safe.
- To change the password, click the same card again. Click **Remove Password** to take it off. The change is saved the next time you save.
- Passwords work for **.pptx**, **.potx**, and **.ppsx** files. An **.odp** file can't hold a password yet, so JeffDeck will not save a protected presentation as .odp. It tells you instead of saving without the password.
- The versions you kept and the backup copy follow the password: they are protected with it, and change when you change or remove it.
- While a presentation has a password, JeffDeck keeps no recovery copy of it, because that copy would not be protected. Save often.

To open a protected file, open it as usual. JeffDeck asks for the password. If it is not right, you can try again, or click **Cancel**. If the file is damaged, JeffDeck says so.

### Inspect Presentation

Click **Inspect Presentation** before you send a presentation to someone. It lists what the file holds that you may not want to share, and how many of each it found:

- **Comments** are notes that people left on the slides.
- **Speaker notes** are the notes you wrote for the person who gives the talk.
- **Document properties and personal information** are the author, the person who last saved it, the company, and the manager.
- **Hidden slides** are slides the slide show skips. **Show All** makes them show again.
- **Content off the slides** is text, pictures, and shapes that lie outside a slide. They do not show, but they are in the file.

Click **Remove All** next to a kind to take it out. You can undo it with **Ctrl+Z**. Save the presentation afterward to keep the clean copy.

### Version History

Click **Version History** to keep named copies of the presentation on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere. Save the presentation first, because a version belongs to a file. Click **Keep a Version…**, and give it a name. Later, choose a version and click **Open a Copy** to look at it, **Go Back to It** to put it in the window (the presentation as it is now is kept as a version first), or **Delete**. JeffDeck keeps the last 50.

### Check Accessibility

Click **Check Accessibility** to open the list of things that may be hard for some people to read or use. It is the same check as **Review > Proofing > Check Accessibility**.

## JeffDeck

- **Options** opens the **JeffDeck Options** window. See [Change JeffDeck's settings](options).
- **About** shows the version of JeffDeck and of Qt, which JeffDeck is built with. It also lists the JeffOffice apps, with links to the **Source code**, the **Downloads**, and **Report a problem**. Each link opens the project's page in your browser. At the bottom are the licence and notices: JeffDeck is free software under the GNU General Public Licence, version 3, and it comes with no warranty.
