# Change JeffDeck's settings

Your settings are in the **JeffDeck Options** window. Click **File > Options** to open it. It has five tabs. Click **OK** to keep your changes, or **Cancel** to throw them away.

## General

- **User name** and **Initials** are shown on the comments you write. See [Comments](comments).
- **Theme** is **Use system setting**, **Light**, or **Dark**. The change shows at once.
- **Display language** is the language of JeffDeck's own words. **Use system language** follows your computer. Other languages appear in this list when translations are available. A new language starts the next time you open JeffDeck.
- **Check for updates when JeffDeck starts** makes JeffDeck look for a newer version each time it starts. If there is one, an **Update Available** window shows what is new. Choose **Update Now**, **Later**, or **Skip This Version**. On Windows, **Update Now** downloads the setup program. It installs the new version of every JeffOffice app, and it asks you to close any that are open. On other computers, the button is **Open Download Page**. It opens the page where you can get the new version. If JeffPub is open too, only one of the two tells you about a new version each day.
- **Get beta versions (early builds for testing)** makes JeffDeck offer beta versions too. These come out before the regular ones. They may have rough edges.
- **Send anonymous usage statistics** lets you share facts about how JeffDeck is used. Anonymous means the facts say nothing about who you are. When this is on, JeffDeck sends these facts once a day, and on the day you update: that it is JeffDeck, its version, your operating system and language, how many times it was started, how often each command is used, and what kind of file you opened or saved (for example, .pptx). If JeffDeck ever closes by surprise, it also sends a crash report the next time it starts. The report says where in the program it stopped, so the problem can be found and fixed. JeffDeck never sends your files, their names, or anything in them, and nothing you typed. If this is off, no crash report is sent either. JeffDeck asks about this the first time it starts, in a window called **Help improve JeffDeck**. It does not ask if you already chose in JeffPub or in setup.
- **Privacy notice** next to that check box opens our privacy notice at jeffoffice.com/privacy. It says what is sent, where it goes, and how long we keep it. We keep statistics for 24 months.
- **Install number** is shown under the check box. It is a random number made up for your copy of JeffOffice, the same in every JeffOffice app. It says nothing about you. Click **Copy** to copy it. If statistics are off, or no number has been made yet, it says **none**.
- To have the statistics we already hold deleted, send your install number to info@jeffoffice.com **before** you turn statistics off. Turning them off erases the number from your computer, and without it we cannot tell which records are yours.
- To turn statistics off without opening JeffDeck, set the environment variable `JP_NO_TELEMETRY` to any value before you start it. Nothing is then counted or sent, and JeffDeck does not ask the first-run question.

**User name**, **Initials**, **Theme**, and **Display language**, and the update and statistics choices, belong to all the JeffOffice apps: change one here and it changes in each of them.

## Proofing

These check boxes control spelling and typing help. See [Type and format text](text).

- **Check spelling as you type**
- **Check grammar as you type** marks clear grammar and style mistakes in English, French, German, and Spanish text with a blue dotted line.
- **Grammar settings** opens a list of the kinds of issues to look for: grammar, punctuation, capitalisation, mixed-up words, repetition, and style (which is off until you turn it on).
- **Ignore words in UPPERCASE**
- **Ignore words that contain numbers**
- **Replace text as you type (AutoCorrect)** fixes common typing mistakes.
- **Replace straight quotes with smart quotes** turns " and ' into curly quotes.
- **AutoFormat as you type (dashes, fractions, ordinals, automatic lists)**
- **Turn web addresses into links as you type** makes a web or email address a link when you press the spacebar after it. Undo takes back just the link.

## View

- **Show the ruler** shows the rulers along the edges of the slide. This is the same as **View > Show > Ruler**, and it applies to every window, now and when you open new ones.
- **Snap objects to other objects** makes an object you drag line up with the other objects on the slide.

## Save

- **Embed fonts in the file** puts the fonts your slides use inside the file when you save, so the slides look the same on a computer that doesn't have those fonts. It is on until you clear it. It only puts in the fonts that come with JeffDeck, and it makes the file bigger. The topic Open and save presentations has the details, under "Fonts in the file".
- **Remove personal information from this file when saving** takes out the author, the person who saved the file last, the company, the manager, and the names, initials, and times on comments, from the file that is written (a .pptx or .odp file, a template, and a presentation sent by email). What the file says stays as it is. The file's custom properties are left as they are, and the document you have open is not changed. It is the same choice in every JeffOffice app: change it in one and it changes in all of them. The same box is on the **Info** page.

## Customise

The **Customise** tab has three pages, **Ribbon**, **Quick Toolbar**, and **Keyboard**. The **Keyboard** page is where you choose the shortcut keys for JeffDeck's commands. See "Choose your own shortcut keys" in the keyboard topic.

### Customise the ribbon

The **Ribbon** page lists the ribbon's tabs, each with its groups.

- **Hide or show a tab or a group.** Clear the box beside it to hide it, and tick the box to show it again. A hidden group takes no room, and its commands still work from their shortcut keys. **Home** has no box, because it always stays, and so does the **File** button. A tab that appears only when something is selected, such as a picture or a table, is at the end of the list and has no box.
- **Make your own tab or group.** Type a name in the **Name** box and click **New Tab**. To make a group, choose a tab and click **New Group**. A group of your own can go on any tab, including a tab that appears only when something is selected.
- **Put commands in your group.** Choose your group, find a command in the **Commands** list (type in the **Search commands** box to narrow the list), and click **Add Command**. Any command that has a name can go in. A command can be in a group only once.
- **Rename.** Choose a tab, group, or command of your own, type the new name, and click **Rename**. A command you rename does the same thing, and the new name is only what its button says. Rename a command with an empty name to give it its own name back.
- **Reorder.** Choose a tab and click **Move Up** or **Move Down**. You can move the ribbon's own tabs as well as yours, but not the tabs that appear only when something is selected. Your groups and your commands move the same way within their tab or group. The ribbon's own groups stay in their order.
- **Take away.** Choose a tab, group, or command of your own and click **Remove**.

**Reset One** puts back the chosen tab: it is shown, its groups are shown, and the groups you added to it are gone. A tab of your own is taken away. **Reset All** puts back the whole ribbon, including the order of the tabs.

Nothing changes until you click **OK**. Then every open JeffDeck window shows your ribbon, and so does every window you open later. JeffDeck keeps only what differs from the usual ribbon, so a tab or group a later version adds shows up on its own. Your tabs and groups get KeyTips (the letters shown when you press **Alt**) that do not clash with the others. You can have 20 tabs of your own, 30 groups of your own on a tab, and 60 commands in a group.

### Customise the quick toolbar

The **Quick Toolbar** page is for the small toolbar at the top left of the window. Its left list shows what is on the toolbar now, and the **Commands** list on the right has every command that has a name (type in the **Search commands** box to narrow it, the same as on the **Ribbon** page).

- **Add a command.** Choose it in the **Commands** list and click **Add Command**. It goes at the end of the toolbar. A command that opens a menu keeps its menu on the toolbar. A command can be on the toolbar only once.
- **Right-click.** You can also right-click any command on the ribbon and choose **Add to Quick Toolbar**, or right-click a button on the toolbar and choose **Remove from Quick Toolbar**. The same menu has **Customise the Quick Toolbar** and **Customise the Ribbon**, which open this page.
- **Move it below the ribbon.** Check **Show Quick Toolbar below the ribbon** to put the toolbar in a row under the ribbon instead of at the top left. The two right-click menus have **Show Quick Toolbar Below the Ribbon** too. JeffDeck remembers the choice.
- **Add a separator.** Click **Add Separator** to put a thin line at the end, then move it where you want it.
- **Reorder.** Choose a command or a separator in the left list and click **Move Up** or **Move Down**.
- **Take away.** Choose it and click **Remove**.

**Reset One** puts back the chosen command: where JeffDeck usually has it, or off the toolbar when JeffDeck does not usually have it there. **Reset All** puts back the toolbar JeffDeck comes with. Reset All on this page does not change your ribbon, and Reset All on the **Ribbon** page does not change your toolbar.

Nothing changes until you click **OK**. Then every open JeffDeck window shows your toolbar, and so does every window you open later. The toolbar can have 100 commands and separators in all. When there are more buttons than fit in a third of the window's width, the rest wait behind a **»** button at the end of the toolbar, so the title of the window is never pushed off the screen. JeffDeck keeps a command in your list when a later version no longer has it, and shows it again if the command comes back. The buttons get KeyTips, the numbers 1 to 9 and then two letters, in the order they are on the toolbar. The toolbar always sits at the top left, beside the **File** button.
