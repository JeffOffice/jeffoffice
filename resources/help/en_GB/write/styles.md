# Use styles
<!-- keywords: manage styles, hide a style, recommended styles, styles, style, styles pane, heading, normal, create style, new style, modify style, change style, based on, next style, update automatically, gallery, character style, paragraph style, ctrl+alt+shift+s -->

A **style** is a saved set of looks: a font, a size, spacing, and more. When you give a style to a paragraph, the paragraph takes all of its looks at once. When you change the style, every paragraph that has it changes too. That is why styles keep long documents tidy.

JeffWrite has two kinds:

- A **paragraph style** sets the look of whole paragraphs. **Heading 1** and **Normal** are paragraph styles.
- A **character style** sets the look of the words you select. **Strong** is a character style.

## Give a style to your text

1. Click in a paragraph, or select the words you want to change.
2. Click a style in the **Styles** gallery on the **Home** tab. Or press **Ctrl+Shift+N** for Normal, or **Ctrl+Alt+1**, **Ctrl+Alt+2**, or **Ctrl+Alt+3** for Heading 1, 2, or 3.

A paragraph style goes to every paragraph you touch. A character style goes to the words you selected.

## Open the Styles pane

Click the small arrow at the corner of the **Styles** group, or press **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+S**. The **Styles** pane opens on the right. It lists the styles of your document, each in its own look.

- The style at your cursor is in bold, with an arrow before its name.
- Click a style to give it to your selection.
- Use the list under the styles to choose which ones to show:
  - **In Use** shows only the styles your text uses.
  - **All Styles** shows every style, including ones your document does not have yet. A style is added to the document the first time you use it.
  - **Recommended** shows the styles that are meant to be seen. A style that is hidden until it is used appears here once you use it.
- Click **Manage…** under the list to choose, for each style, whether it is **Recommended** (shown in the gallery), whether it is **Hidden** (left out of every list), and its **Order in the lists**. A lower number comes first. Click **OK**, and all the changes happen in one step, so one **Undo** takes them back.
- In **Manage…**, click **Delete** to remove a style you made. Paragraphs that had it go back to Normal, and the styles that were based on it are based on what it was based on. Styles that come with JeffWrite can't be deleted. Deleting is one step, so **Undo** brings the style back.
- In **Manage…**, click **Import from file…** to copy styles from another document. Pick the file, check the styles you want, and click **OK**. A style that needs another style brings it along. Styles you already have are left alone unless you check **Replace styles that have the same name**. Importing is one step to undo.
- Under the list, the **Sort** box puts the styles in order. **Alphabetical** goes by name. **As Recommended** puts the most useful styles first. **By Type** lists paragraph styles, then linked styles, then character styles. **Based On** groups each style with the style it is built on.

## Make a style from your text

1. Format a paragraph the way you want it. For example, make it bold, centre it, and set the size.
2. Click **New Style** at the bottom of the pane, or click **Create a Style** on the **Home** tab.
3. Type a **Name**. A name can have up to 255 letters, and it cannot be the name of another style.
4. Check the other choices, and click **OK**.

The new style is saved with your document, and your paragraph takes it. One **Undo** (Ctrl+Z) takes the style back.

## Change a style

1. Click in a paragraph that has the style. Or click the style in the pane.
2. Click **Modify** at the bottom of the pane, or click **Modify Style** on the **Home** tab.
3. Change what you want, and click **OK**.

The dialog has these choices:

- **Name** is the name of the style. A style that comes with JeffWrite keeps its name.
- **Based on** is the style this one starts from. The new style looks like that style, except for what you change. If you change the base style later, this style follows.
- **Style for the following paragraph** is the style the next paragraph gets when you press **Enter** at the end of this one. A heading is usually followed by Normal.
- **Font**, **size**, **Bold**, **Italic**, **Underline**, and **colour** set how the words look.
- **Alignment**, **indentation**, **spacing**, and **line spacing** set how paragraphs look.
- **Add to the style gallery** puts the style in the gallery on the **Home** tab.
- **Update automatically** makes the style follow your formatting. When you change a paragraph that has this style, the style changes to match, and so do all the other paragraphs that have it.

When you change a style, the paragraphs that have it change. If you gave a paragraph a look of its own, such as one red word, it keeps that look.

If you click **OK** without changing anything, the document is not changed.

## Update a style to match your text

Format a paragraph the way you want. Then click **Update Style to Match Selection** on the **Home** tab. The style of that paragraph takes on the changes you made, and every paragraph with that style follows.
